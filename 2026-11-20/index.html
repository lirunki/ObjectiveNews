Anthropic closes first trading day at $97.17 after record IPO

Anthropic shares opened at $111.50 on 19 November and closed at $97.17, a 17 percent gain over the $83 offering price that raised $81.3 billion. The fully diluted valuation settled near $1.77 trillion. Reuters, AP and PBS match on the close and the size ranking above SpaceX’s June listing. Market commentary about bubble risk is framing; the open, close and proceeds are checkable against exchange data and the prospectus.

Senate passes bipartisan energy permitting bill 62-33

The Senate approved the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act on 19 November by 62-33. Fourteen Democrats and one independent joined most Republicans. The bill shortens deadlines for environmental reviews under NEPA and other statutes and expands federal authority over interstate transmission. It now goes to the House. Reuters, Roll Call and Axios agree on the vote and the core mechanisms. Claims that it “solves affordability” are aspirational; the vote total is recorded.

Trump returns from Shenzhen trilateral; White House press conference set

Trump landed back in the United States after the 19 November meeting with Xi and Putin. The three leaders issued a declaration reaffirming that nuclear war must never be fought, calling for UN reform and more petroleum-product supplies. No new arms-control talks or Ukraine settlement mechanism were announced. Kushner later said Russia was “comfortable” with NATO-like guarantees for Ukraine. Reuters and AP match on the declaration text and the absence of breakthroughs. “Historic peace” language is a claim, not an independent verification of enforcement.

Encuestas flash sitúan a Feijóo como ganador del cara a cara con Sánchez

El debate del 19 de noviembre entre Pedro Sánchez y Alberto Núñez Feijóo reunió más de 10 millones de espectadores. Encuestas flash de 40dB para El País y la SER dieron a Feijóo un 43 % frente al 33,3 % de Sánchez; SocioMétrica para El Español reportó 64 % a 32 %. ElDiario.es situó al líder del PP 13 puntos por delante. Las cifras de audiencia y los porcentajes de las encuestas son verificables en los comunicados de los medios. Que “ganó el debate” es una percepción de muestra, no un resultado electoral.
