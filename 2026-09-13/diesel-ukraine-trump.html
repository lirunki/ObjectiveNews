Energy · 13 September 2026

What is known about record U.S. diesel and Trump’s call for Ukraine to spare Russian refineries

Neutral reconstruction from wire reporting. Not an endorsement of any official’s causal story.

On Thursday 10 September 2026, price trackers recorded the first national U.S. retail diesel average at or above $6 a gallon. GasBuddy put the print at $6.00; AAA’s Friday reading was $6.0556. A year earlier the AAA average was about $3.71.

On Sunday 13 September at the Irish Open in Doonbeg, President Trump said Volodymyr Zelenskyy “has to stop knocking out diesel fuel in Russia,” and that the shortage “isn’t done by the Middle East.” Reuters, AP and POLITICO Europe carried matching quotations. Two supply shocks are visible at once: Ukrainian strikes on Russian refining, and Middle East disruption from the U.S.–Iran war. Pump data are findings. Sole blame on Ukraine is a presidential claim.
