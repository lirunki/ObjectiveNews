Wisconsin · 12–13 September 2026

A congressman and a pilot swam away from a small-plane lake landing. That is the whole confirmed story so far.

AP / PBS account. No cause finding yet.

On Saturday night 12 September 2026, U.S. Rep. Tom Tiffany, a Wisconsin Republican, was a passenger in a small plane that made an emergency landing in a Wisconsin lake. Tiffany and the pilot got out, swam, called 911, and were taken off the water by a fire-department airboat. Associated Press reporting carried by PBS and other subscribers said both were safe. No passenger list beyond those two has been established in the same wires. The National Transportation Safety Board process, if opened, had not produced a probable-cause statement by Sunday midday Pacific time.

There is no verified public evidence in the first-cycle stories that this was anything other than a general-aviation emergency. Outlets that fold the item into campaign or culture-war copy are adding a frame the facts do not require. The only numbers that belong here until investigators speak are: one aircraft, two people known to have been aboard, zero reported serious injuries, one airboat rescue.
