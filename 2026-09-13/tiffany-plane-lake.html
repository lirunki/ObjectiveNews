Wisconsin · Accident · 12–13 September 2026

Two people left a sinking light aircraft in Lake Wausau. The mechanical cause is not yet public.

ObjectiveNews reconstruction from sheriff, hospital-release, and wire accounts. Target scores for this page: T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

Late Saturday 12 September 2026, a single-engine Beechcraft K35 (Bonanza family) approaching Wausau Downtown Airport lost power. The pilot, Leonard Boltz of Pearson, Wisconsin, put the airplane on Lake Wausau. Two people were aboard: Boltz and U.S. Rep. Tom Tiffany (R-WI-7), Republican nominee for governor of Wisconsin. They were returning from the La Crosse County Lincoln Day Dinner.

Marathon County Sheriff’s Office said the 911 call came at 8:49 p.m. local time. As the airframe began to submerge they left it, swam to a shallow area, and waited. A Wausau Fire Department airboat reached them at about 9:05 p.m. Both men were treated for minor injuries and released the same night. Tiffany received stitches above the right eye. Cause is not yet public.

The event is not, by itself, evidence about fitness for office. Democrat David Crowley issued a short statement of relief and credited the pilot. No official has alleged pilot error or sabotage.
