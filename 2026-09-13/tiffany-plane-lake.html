Wisconsin · 12–13 September 2026

A congressman and a pilot swam away from a small-plane lake landing. That is the whole confirmed story so far.

AP / PBS / BBC accounts. No official cause finding yet.

On Saturday night 12 September 2026, U.S. Rep. Tom Tiffany, a Wisconsin Republican and the party’s nominee for governor, was a passenger in a single-engine Beechcraft K35 that lost power approaching Wausau Downtown Airport. The pilot, Leonard Boltz, put the aircraft down in Lake Wausau. Both men called 911, left the plane as it began to submerge, swam to shallow water, and were taken off by a Wausau Fire Department airboat about 15–20 minutes after the 8:49 p.m. call. Both were treated for minor injuries (Tiffany received stitches above one eye) and released the same night.

There is no verified public evidence in the first-cycle wires that this was anything other than a general-aviation emergency. Until investigators publish a cause, the confirmed numbers are: one aircraft, two people aboard, zero reported serious injuries, one airboat rescue.
