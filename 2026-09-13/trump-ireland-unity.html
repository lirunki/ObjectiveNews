Diplomacy · Ireland / United Kingdom · 12 September 2026

Trump said a united Ireland would be “fantastic.” That is a preference, not a treaty change.

ObjectiveNews. Target scores: T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

On Saturday 12 September 2026 President Donald Trump, sitting with Taoiseach Micheál Martin in Dublin, said he would “love to see it unified,” that it would be “fantastic,” that “it’s going to happen eventually,” and that “it may as well happen now.” He added that the United Kingdom “will have something to say about it, obviously.”

Those sentences are on the record. They are not a change to the Good Friday Agreement. The 1998 settlement still requires consent of a majority in Northern Ireland before status changes. Downing Street restated that a referendum is not on the table. Unionist parties rejected the prediction.
