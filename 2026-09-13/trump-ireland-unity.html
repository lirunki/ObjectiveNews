Diplomacy · 12–13 September 2026

Trump said Irish unification would be “fantastic.” That is a comment, not a change in the Good Friday rules.

Quotations from Reuters, BBC and Irish Times. The 1998 Agreement is not amended by a U.S. remark.

After talks at Farmleigh on 12 September, Trump was asked about a united Ireland. Reuters recorded: he would “love to see it unified,” that “it’s going to happen eventually,” and that it would be “a fantastic thing.” He added that “the UK will have something to say.” On Sunday in Clare he called Saturday’s answer “a fairly routine comment.”

Those sentences break with recent U.S. practice of not endorsing an outcome. They do not rewrite the Good Friday Agreement. Downing Street said a referendum remains off the table. A Taoiseach spokesperson said Irish policy had not changed.
