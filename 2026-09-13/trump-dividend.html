What Trump actually promised: $5,000 if Republicans keep both chambers

The sentence was spoken in Dallas on 9 September. Everything after that is arithmetic, law, or someone else’s gloss.

At the Republican midterm convention in Dallas on 9 September 2026, President Trump said that if Republicans win both the House and the Senate he will issue a $5,000 “dividend” to every adult U.S. citizen, to be spent in the United States.

Sending $5,000 to every adult citizen would cost on the order of $1.2–1.35 trillion. The President cannot appropriate that by speech. Vice President Vance later said the wealthy would be excluded and pointed at tariff revenue. Tariff collections to date are far smaller than $1 trillion.

A campaign promise of future legislation is not the same legal object as handing cash at a polling place. House Speaker Johnson said Congress would have to approve any such payment.
