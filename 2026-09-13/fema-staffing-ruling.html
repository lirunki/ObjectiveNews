Judge Illston: DHS may not order FEMA to cut half its staff

U.S. District Judge Susan Illston ruled late Friday 11 September 2026 that DHS acted unlawfully when it directed FEMA toward a 50 percent workforce cut and pulled FEMA’s power to renew CORE on-call disaster employees.

Post-Katrina statute says DHS may not substantially reduce FEMA’s authorities. Illston wrote that the 50 percent target appears as if pulled from thin air. The full cut was not executed. The Friday opinion did not set a complete remedy.
