Judge Illston: DHS may not order FEMA to cut half its staff

Judge Susan Illston held that DHS acted unlawfully in directing a 50% FEMA cut and in taking CORE renewal authority. Post-Katrina law bars DHS from substantially reducing FEMA’s functions. The full cut was not executed. The Friday opinion did not set a complete remedy.
