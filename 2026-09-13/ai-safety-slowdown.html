What AI executives actually proposed this weekend—and what they did not

A reconstruction of public statements, not a verdict on catastrophe scenarios.

On Saturday 12 September, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay of about 3,800 words. The documented core is not “stop building AI.” It is: capability is compounding faster than evaluation; models are beginning to help build the next models; labs should slow the rate of capability jumps so evaluation can catch up.

Three steps, as reported by the New York Times, BBC and The Verge: (1) independent embedded evaluators inside labs; (2) common safety standards and a capability speed limit among firms in democratic states; (3) attempt analogous limits that also bind China, treated as least likely near-term.

Sam Altman posted that he agreed a slowdown was needed. Elon Musk and Demis Hassabis voiced agreement with the direction. No lab announced a numerical freeze on training FLOPs. Delaying an IPO is not the same thing as a training pause.
