United States · prices · BLS release of 11 September 2026

August consumer prices rose 0.4% in a month and 3.4% in a year

Written to official BLS tables.

The Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that CPI-U rose 0.4 percent in August after seasonal adjustment, after 0.1 percent in July. Over twelve months the all-items index is up 3.4 percent. Core CPI rose 0.3 percent in the month and 2.4 percent over the year.

Gasoline rose 3.9 percent in August and accounted for more than one-third of the monthly increase. Energy rose 2.1 percent in the month and 16.3 percent over twelve months. Gasoline is up 27.4 percent year over year; fuel oil 52.0 percent. Food rose 0.1 percent in August and 2.7 percent over the year. Shelter rose 0.3 percent in the month and 3.0 percent over the year.

Year-over-year headline inflation was 3.4 percent in both July and August. The new information is the monthly acceleration and the composition. Energy chronology includes the Iran war after February 2026. That is chronology, not a verdict on the war or on the next Fed meeting.
