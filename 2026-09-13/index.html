Energy · Ukraine · U.S. prices

Record U.S. diesel and Trump’s demand that Ukraine stop striking Russian refineries

U.S. retail diesel crossed $6 a gallon for the first time late last week. On Sunday in Ireland, President Trump said Volodymyr Zelenskyy “has to stop knocking out diesel fuel in Russia.” Ukraine calls refineries legitimate military targets; several European officials say both wars are tightening product markets.

Technology

AI chiefs call for a slower frontier

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei published an essay Saturday arguing that capability growth is outrunning safety work. Sam Altman and Elon Musk said they agreed in principle.

Courts

Judge: DHS plan to cut FEMA staff by 50% was unlawful

Judge Susan Illston held that DHS unlawfully directed FEMA toward a 50% workforce cut. She called the 50% figure as if “pulled from thin air.”

Diplomacy

Trump says Irish unification would be “fantastic”

During a two-day Ireland trip, President Trump broke with the traditional U.S. posture of not taking a side on unification. Downing Street said a referendum is not on the table.

España · Migración

Ceuta: 5.321 retornos según el Gobierno; la ciudad habla de miles aún allí

El ministro Ángel Víctor Torres anunció 5.321 regresos voluntarios a Marruecos. El gobierno local estima alrededor de 13.000 personas todavía en la ciudad.

Accident

Rep. Tom Tiffany swims ashore after a lake landing

The Wisconsin congressman and the pilot swam to safety Saturday after an emergency landing in Lake Wausau. Minor injuries only. Cause not yet determined.
