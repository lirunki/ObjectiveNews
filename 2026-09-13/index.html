Prices

August CPI-U rose 0.4% on the month and 3.4% on the year

The Bureau of Labor Statistics released the August Consumer Price Index on 11 September. The seasonally adjusted all-items index rose 0.4 percent from July. The not-seasonally-adjusted 12-month change was 3.4 percent. Energy was +16.3 percent over the year; gasoline was +27.4 percent. Core CPI was +0.3 percent on the month and +2.4 percent on the year. Those are survey measurements, not a Federal Reserve decision. Markets treated the print as leaving a 16 September FOMC meeting live. A year-over-year energy spike and a core print above some 0.2 percent month forecasts are different sentences.

Midterms

$5,000 adult dividend restated; no statute exists

On Sunday in Doonbeg, President Trump repeated a pledge first made at the Republican midterm convention in Dallas: $5,000 to every adult if Republicans keep the House and Senate. He said it is easy if the Republicans win and that Democrats cannot make that pledge. Senator Bernie Moreno said he would prepare a bill after 3 November. Speaker Johnson has not published a text. The checkable fact is the spoken condition. A speech is not an appropriation.

Courts

Judge Illston: DHS 50% FEMA staffing plan was unlawful

U.S. District Judge Susan Illston issued an opinion late Friday on a labor suit against DHS. She wrote that DHS unlawfully usurped FEMA’s authority over personnel and that a directed 50 percent staffing plan lacked reasoned decision-making. Coverage quotes her on the 50 percent figure looking pulled from thin air. The Post-Katrina Act says DHS may not substantially reduce FEMA’s functions. The 50 percent cut was not fully executed. The court told the parties to confer on relief. A holding of unlawfulness is not a staffing table for hurricane season.

Energy / Ukraine

Diesel near $6; Trump tells Kyiv to stop hitting Russian diesel

AAA and GasBuddy printed a U.S. retail diesel average just over $6 a gallon late last week, a record on those series. That is a price. On Sunday in Doonbeg, Trump said Volodymyr Zelenskyy has to stop knocking out diesel fuel in Russia because that is hurting the world, and that the shortage is not done by the Middle East. Ukraine says refineries that fund and fuel Russia’s war are legitimate targets. Russia banned diesel exports in July after domestic rationing. A second supply story — conflict affecting the Strait of Hormuz — is also on the same tape. The president’s sentence is a request plus a causal claim. The pump average does not, by itself, identify a single cause.

Technology

Amodei asks labs to pace frontier models; Trump says the winner of AI wins

On Saturday Dario Amodei published a roughly 3,800-word essay, reported under the title We Must Pace the Frontier. He asked for slower capability growth, embedded evaluators, allied standards, and — least likely, he said — coordination that includes China. BBC and the Times reported public agreement from Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and Demis Hassabis. On Sunday Trump, asked about slowing or regulating, said he did not want to cede an edge to China and that whoever wins with AI wins. No new statute was signed. No third-party audit of a named model was attached to the letter. A CEO essay and a presidential aside are not the same object as a measured accident.

Diplomacy

Trump calls Irish unification fantastic; Good Friday still requires consent

In Dublin on Saturday, sitting with Taoiseach Micheál Martin, Trump said he would love to see it unified and that unification would be fantastic. He said it would happen eventually and may as well happen now, while adding that the UK will have something to say about it. On Sunday in Doonbeg he repeated that putting the two jurisdictions together was one of the naturals, and declined to talk about Scotland yet. The 1998 Good Friday Agreement lets the UK government call a border poll if it appears likely that a majority in Northern Ireland would vote to join the Republic. Downing Street, under Prime Minister Andy Burnham in this week’s reporting, said that test has not been met. A visitor’s preference is not a poll writ.

España

Ceuta: el Gobierno cuenta 5.321 retornos; la ciudad habla de miles que siguen

El ministro Ángel Víctor Torres dijo este domingo que 5.321 personas han retornado voluntariamente a Marruecos desde el 10 de agosto y que unas 90 han renunciado al asilo. A primera hora, 864 personas que dormían junto a Loma Colmenar fueron trasladadas a un recinto en Los Rosales. El Ejecutivo cifra en 4.500 las plazas estatales y en unos 2.000 los menores a cargo de la Ciudad Autónoma. Las autoridades locales, citadas por EFE, estiman todavía en torno a 13.000 personas en la ciudad. No es un padrón publicado. La entrada de los días 30 y 31 de julio se describe en cables como 70.000–80.000 personas, la mayoría vueltas en horas. La cifra de Torres es un recuento administrativo; la de Ceuta es una estimación política.

Accident

Rep. Tom Tiffany and pilot swim from a Lake Wausau landing

Marathon County said a 911 call came at 20:49 Saturday that a plane was in Lake Wausau. A Wausau Fire Department airboat recovered two people about 21:05. They were Rep. Tom Tiffany, Republican of Wisconsin and the party’s nominee for governor, and pilot Leonard Boltz. Tiffany wrote that the Beechcraft K35 lost power on approach to Wausau Downtown Airport, that they called 911 after landing, and that they swam to shallows as the aircraft began to submerge. Both were treated for minor injuries and released; his campaign said he received stitches above the right eye. Cause is described as a loss of power. That is a preliminary account, not an NTSB probable-cause report.
