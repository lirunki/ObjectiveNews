Rep. Tom Tiffany and pilot swam after Beechcraft landed in Lake Wausau

A single-engine Beechcraft lost power on approach to Wausau Downtown Airport around 20:45 local time on 12 September. The pilot, Leonard Boltz, put the aircraft onto Lake Wausau; both men exited as it submerged and swam to a sandbar before an airboat recovery at approximately 21:05. Marathon County Sheriff and Wausau Fire Department times match across AP, BBC, NPR and local outlets. Tiffany, the Republican nominee for Wisconsin governor, received stitches for a cut above the eye and said the campaign continues. The NTSB has not released a probable-cause finding; the airframe type is a Bonanza, not a jet or a commercial airliner. No third-party injury or property damage on shore has been reported.

August CPI-U rose 0.4 percent month-over-month and 3.4 percent year-over-year

BLS published the August 2026 CPI-U on 11 September. Seasonally adjusted all-items rose 0.4 percent from July; the 12-month change was 3.4 percent. Core (all items less food and energy) rose 0.3 percent on the month and 2.4 percent on the year. Energy was +16.3 percent year-over-year, driven largely by gasoline (+27.4 percent) and fuel oil (+52.0 percent). Food was +2.7 percent year-over-year. Shelter remained near 3.0 percent. These are the published index values; they do not themselves dictate a Federal Reserve rate decision. Market commentary over the weekend treated the print as consistent with consensus on the headline and modestly firmer than some core guesses.

Federal judge rules DHS plan to cut FEMA staffing by half unlawful

U.S. District Judge Susan Illston (N.D. Cal.) held on Friday that a Department of Homeland Security directive requiring FEMA to plan for an approximately 50 percent workforce reduction was arbitrary and capricious and violated post-Katrina statutory limits on DHS’s ability to reduce FEMA’s functions. The opinion states the 11,000-plus cut figure “appears as if pulled from thin air.” Remedies and any injunction language are deferred to a later hearing. The ruling does not itself rehire any specific employee; it addresses the legality of the planning directive. DHS and FEMA had not issued a public response by Sunday afternoon Pacific time.

Trump restates $5,000 ‘dividend’ idea; Speaker Johnson says Congress must approve

President Trump described a $5,000 per-adult payment as “easy” to fit into the federal budget if Republicans retain or gain control after the midterms. House Speaker Mike Johnson stated publicly that any such transfer would require congressional authorization and appropriation. No bill text has been introduced. The arithmetic of a universal $5,000 adult payment exceeds $1 trillion; the White House has not released an offset schedule. The statement is a campaign-period proposal, not an enacted program and not a signed executive order that moves money.

Trump urges Ukraine to halt strikes on Russian diesel facilities

President Trump publicly called on Ukraine to stop attacking Russian diesel and fuel infrastructure, stating the strikes were contributing to shortages. Ukrainian forces have conducted repeated drone and missile strikes on Russian refineries and storage in recent months; independent commercial trackers show reduced Russian product exports on some routes. The statement does not alter existing U.S. security assistance authorities and does not itself stop any Ukrainian operation. Diesel prices in Europe and the United States remain influenced by multiple factors including the broader energy market and residual effects of earlier Red Sea and Gulf disruptions.

Trump downplays need to slow AI development relative to China

In weekend remarks the President said he does not want the United States to cede an edge in artificial intelligence to China and attributed some calls for slower development to “negative forces.” He acknowledged that some regulation may be appropriate but offered no legislative text or executive-order language. Separate statements from House leadership indicated bipartisan interest in beginning regulatory talks. No new statute or binding agency rule was announced. The remarks are framing for a policy debate that has not yet produced enacted constraints on model training or deployment.

Trump in Ireland calls Irish reunification ‘fantastic’

During a weekend visit centered on a golf event, President Trump met Irish officials and stated that reunification of the island would be “fantastic.” The remark touched a long-standing and sensitive constitutional question under the Good Friday Agreement framework. Irish government spokespeople noted the comment without adopting it as policy. No change in U.S. diplomatic posture or in Northern Ireland’s constitutional status followed. The statement is rhetorical; the legal path to any border poll remains internal to the United Kingdom and Ireland under existing treaties.

Gobierno español defiende su gestión de la crisis migratoria en Ceuta

El presidente del Gobierno, Pedro Sánchez, insistió ante el Parlamento que no existían avisos previos solidarios sobre la magnitud de la entrada masiva en Ceuta ocurrida en julio. La oposición (PP y Vox) acusa al Ejecutivo de abandono de la ciudad autónoma y exige responsabilidades. Miles de personas ya han retornado a Marruecos; varios miles permanecen en Ceuta bajo gestión de las autoridades españolas. Fiscalía y fuerzas de seguridad han reportado agresiones sexuales y altercados en los días posteriores al pico. No hay, a esta fecha, una sentencia judicial que atribuya la coordinación de la entrada a un gobierno extranjero; las investigaciones policiales continúan.
