Choque de trenes de alta velocidad cerca de Toledo deja al menos 64 muertos

El Gobierno de Castilla-La Mancha confirmó al menos 64 fallecidos tras la colisión por alcance entre un AVE de Renfe y un servicio Iryo en el kilómetro 118 de la línea Madrid-Andalucía, cerca de Urda (Toledo). Las agencias coinciden en que el AVE impactó un Iryo detenido, con varios vagones descarrilados y caídos por un terraplén. Había 544 pasajeros en total. Las hipótesis iniciales apuntan a un paso de señal en rojo y posible fallo del sistema LZB más error humano; no hay informe técnico oficial aún. Es el segundo gran accidente en la misma línea este año, tras Adamuz en enero.

Netanyahu bloc leads Israeli election count but falls short of majority

With more than 70 percent of votes counted, Israeli media projected Netanyahu’s right-wing and ultra-Orthodox bloc at about 54 seats, the Eisenkot-led opposition at about 48, and Arab parties at about 19 in the 120-seat Knesset. Turnout reached 77.7 percent, the highest in more than two decades. Wires agree no bloc has a clear path to 61 seats without cross-aisle deals that many have ruled out. Netanyahu remains caretaker prime minister during negotiations. Trump credited his endorsement for the result; that is a claim, not an independent causal finding.

Federal Reserve expected to hold rates steady after September hike

Markets priced roughly a 90 percent chance that the FOMC would leave the federal funds rate in the 3.75–4.00 percent range on 28 October, after raising it 25 basis points in September. Reuters, AP and others report that inflation remains above the 2 percent target and that officials have signaled patience ahead of midterm elections. No Summary of Economic Projections or updated dot plot is expected. Chair Kevin Warsh’s press conference will be the main source of new information on the December path. The hold is a consensus expectation, not yet an announced decision.

Trump highlights Venezuelan oil tanker and methane-rule rollback at Texas rally

At a campaign event in Texas on 27 October, President Trump pointed to a tanker he said carried one million barrels of Venezuelan oil and announced the termination of a 2023 Biden-era methane regulation on oil and gas operations. AP and Reuters report the EPA proposal is expected to save costs and increase production volumes according to the administration. The tanker arrival and volume are administration statements; independent verification of the exact cargo was not published in the initial wires. The midterm context is that gas prices remain elevated after the Iran conflict.
