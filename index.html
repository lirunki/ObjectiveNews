How scores work

Truthfulness (0–100)
How closely the linked piece matches independently checkable facts (dates, quotes, court holdings, official numbers). Opinion does not lower T if it is labeled as opinion.
Neutrality (−100 SuperLeft … +100 SuperRight)
Framing and loaded wording, not the author’s voter registration. 0 is descriptive wire style.
Complicity (0 Super-complicit … 100 none)
Whether the outlet treats a speaker’s unverified or contested claim as established fact about the world.
