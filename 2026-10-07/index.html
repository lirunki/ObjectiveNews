Henri Kagan and Kenso Soai won the chemistry Nobel

The Academy’s citation is the decision: non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis. Reuters, the BBC and the press release agree on Kagan, Université Paris-Sud, Soai, Tokyo University of Science, and the mirror-image problem. The press page dates Kagan’s step to 1986 and Soai’s success to 2003 after a 1995 paper. The BBC’s prize line is 12.0 million kronor, about $1.25 million. Soai’s shopping-trip sentence is a phone call, not a new experiment. The still is an older Academy-associated exterior, not the Stockholm press conference.

The White House defended a Trump line about Los Angeles and San Diego

The sentence was said in Nebraska on 5 October. On 6–7 October, communications director Steven Cheung said the line meant U.S. cities would have been threatened if a nuclear Iran had not been stopped. Governor Newsom read it as an invitation. Neither reading is a strike order. No piece here shows an Iranian statement naming the cities on Monday. The still is a 2019 city-hall exterior, not the rally.
