The step is a halt on new and pending PERM labor certifications, not a proven fraud judgment. Vance’s 6,000 layoffs, 6,300 H-1B visas, and nearly 3,000 green cards are his figures. Reuters notes Microsoft’s July disclosure of 4,800 job cuts. Those numbers are not reconciled. Fox’s headline treats abuse as the event. The still is a campus type photo.
