Warren Buffett steps down as Berkshire Hathaway chairman; son Howard succeeds

Berkshire Hathaway stated on 18 September that Warren Buffett ceases to serve as chairman and becomes chairman emeritus, remaining a director. Howard G. Buffett, a director since 1993, becomes chairman. Greg Abel has been CEO since the start of 2026. The company’s letter and statement are primary; wires match the effective-immediately timing and the succession names. No change in operating control of the subsidiaries is claimed. The move completes a succession plan first outlined publicly in 2025. Share price reaction was muted on the day of the announcement.

LA prosecutors charge SUV driver with murder in bus crash that preceded helicopter fatalities

Los Angeles County District Attorney Nathan Hochman announced charges of two counts of second-degree murder against Bailee Lynn Rios, 36, in the deaths of Metro bus passengers Daniel Castillo, 46, and Gage Weida, 31. Prosecutors allege she was impaired by drugs, drove the wrong way, struck another vehicle, ran a red light and collided with the bus on 15 September in Chatsworth. A news helicopter covering the scene later crashed, killing three. Bail is set at $4.1 million; arraignment scheduled for 18 September. The helicopter crash is a separate incident under investigation; the murder charges address only the bus deaths.

Policía inicia el traslado de migrantes desde las playas de Ceuta al puerto

Agentes de policía comenzaron el 18 de septiembre a trasladar a cientos de migrantes acampados en playas de Ceuta hacia instalaciones temporales en la zona portuaria, tras la autorización judicial de uso provisional. El inicio del operativo registró una estampida y disparos de advertencia al aire; varias personas resultaron heridas. La capacidad declarada del nuevo dispositivo es de aproximadamente 1.700 plazas. Las cifras de permanencia siguen divergiendo: la ciudad autónom a habla de unos 13.000 y el Gobierno central de una cifra inferior. El contexto es el cruce masivo de finales de julio. El artículo completo está en español.

Early in-person voting begins in Virginia for November 2026 midterms

Virginia’s Department of Elections confirmed that early in-person voting for the 3 November 2026 general election opened on 18 September in localities whose registrar offices would otherwise be closed on the 19th. The official 45-day window runs through 31 October. Voters will decide a U.S. Senate seat, all 11 House districts, three proposed state constitutional amendments and local contests. New for 2026 is mandatory Sunday voting on the second and third Sundays before Election Day. Mail-ballot rules remain subject to ongoing litigation; the early-vote opening itself is an administrative fact reported by the state agency.
