Los Angeles · criminal charges · 18 September 2026

Los Angeles prosecutors charge the SUV driver with two counts of second-degree murder in the Metro bus collision.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

On or about 17–18 September 2026, Los Angeles County District Attorney Nathan Hochman announced that Bailee Lynn Rios, 36, had been charged with two counts of second-degree murder in connection with the deaths of two passengers on a Los Angeles Metro bus. The passengers were identified by the medical examiner as Daniel Castillo, 46, and Gage Weida, 31. The collision occurred on Tuesday, 15 September, in the Chatsworth neighborhood of the San Fernando Valley.

Prosecutors allege that Rios was operating a 2004 Ford Expedition while under the influence of drugs, drove the wrong way on a busy avenue, struck another vehicle, ran a red light, and collided with the Metro bus. Rios was held on $4.1 million bail and was scheduled to appear in court on 18 September.

Separately, a news helicopter that had been covering the bus collision crashed later the same evening, killing the pilot, George Marciniw; the reporter/photojournalist, Eliana Moreno; and a person on the ground, Edy Gutierrez Mejia, 29. The helicopter crash is under its own investigation. The murder charges announced by the district attorney address only the two bus-passenger deaths.

What is only a quote or allegation

The assertion that Rios was impaired by drugs is a prosecutorial allegation grounded in toxicology or related evidence that has not yet been fully tested in open court. The precise sequence of lane changes, speeds, and traffic-signal status will be subjects of expert and witness testimony.

Mechanism and context a reader needs

Under California law, second-degree murder does not require premeditation. A charge of second-degree murder in a vehicular context typically rests on a theory of implied malice: that the defendant acted with conscious disregard for human life. Whether the evidence of impairment, wrong-way driving, and red-light running will satisfy that standard is a question for the court and, if the case proceeds, for a jury.

Los Angeles Metro operates a large surface-bus fleet under the Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority. News helicopters routinely orbit major traffic collisions in the Los Angeles basin. The subsequent crash of the Angel City Air / NBC4 aircraft is the subject of a separate National Transportation Safety Board and local investigation.

What the story is not

The murder charges do not adjudicate guilt. They are formal accusations that initiate the criminal process. The helicopter fatalities are not charged against Rios in the announcements reviewed; any potential liability theories connecting the two events would require separate legal analysis.
