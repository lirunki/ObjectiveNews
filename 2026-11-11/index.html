House votes 211-204 to refer Hegseth impeachment articles to committee

The House on Tuesday voted 211-204 on a procedural motion to send the eight articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, introduced by Rep. Thomas Massie, to the Republican-led Judiciary Committee. Massie and Democrats supported the articles focused on the Iran war and related operations; 11 Democrats were absent. President Trump and Vice President Vance publicly backed Hegseth, who called the effort a farce. The referral delays any floor vote on the articles themselves until after the current Congress ends. Wires agree on the vote total, the referral mechanism, and the filer; whether the articles will advance under the next Democratic majority is a future procedural question.

House Republicans elect Jim Jordan as minority leader 106-73

In a closed-door secret ballot on Tuesday, House Republicans elected Rep. Jim Jordan of Ohio as their leader for the next Congress, defeating Rep. Steve Scalise of Louisiana 106-73. Jordan, a Freedom Caucus co-founder and Judiciary Committee chair, will lead the party in the minority after midterm losses. Senate Republicans re-elected John Thune. The vote is an internal party selection for the leadership post that takes effect when the new Congress convenes; it does not alter the current majority status until January. Wires match on the candidates, the tally, and the context of preparing for minority status.

DOJ charges three in alleged Iran-backed plots including RAF Fairford

The Justice Department unsealed an indictment charging three men—Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood Al Saadi, Mohsen Mohajeri, and Mohammad Ali Al Hassani—with terrorism-related offenses tied to an Iran-aligned network involving Kataib Hezbollah and the IRGC. Prosecutors allege involvement in nearly 20 attacks or attempts in Europe and the US, including a September plot against RAF Fairford where US aircraft and personnel were based. The UK has attributed the Fairford incident to Iran. The charges are formal allegations; the defendants' current locations and trial dates are not detailed in the initial releases. Huelga general del 11N en España por la vivienda: primera conjunta de UGT y CCOO en 14 años

Los sindicatos UGT y CCOO convocaron una huelga general de 24 horas el 11 de noviembre, la primera conjunta desde 2012, bajo el lema salarios, vivienda y precios, en protesta por la crisis habitacional. Los sindicatos reportaron millones de participantes y paros elevados en puertos, automoción y otros sectores; se registraron interrupciones en transportes y algunos enfrentamientos. El Banco de España ha citado un déficit de unas 750.000 viviendas. Las cifras de participación son estimaciones sindicales; los servicios mínimos y el impacto electoral a menos de tres semanas de las generales del 29 de noviembre son hechos verificables por separado. La fotografía es del Palacio de Cibeles, sede del Ayuntamiento de Madrid, no de una manifestación concreta.
