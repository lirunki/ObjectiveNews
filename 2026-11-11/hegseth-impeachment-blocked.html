Washington · Defense · 11 November 2026

House votes 211-204 to refer Hegseth impeachment articles to the Judiciary Committee.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

On 10 November 2026 the House of Representatives voted 211 to 204 on a motion to refer eight articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to the House Judiciary Committee. The articles had been introduced the previous day by Rep. Thomas Massie, Republican of Kentucky, as a privileged resolution. Massie was the only Republican to vote against the referral; eleven Democrats were recorded as absent. The motion succeeded under the existing Republican majority, which ends when the new Congress convenes in January.

The articles accuse Hegseth of high crimes and misdemeanors related to the conduct of military operations against Iran, strikes on boats in the Caribbean, and the capture of Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro. Hegseth held a Pentagon press briefing on 10 November and described the resolution as a farce and a political spectacle. President Trump posted that the effort had failed and that Hegseth’s record warranted support. Vice President JD Vance also stated that Hegseth had not committed impeachable offenses.

What is only a quote

Statements that the Iran operations were illegal or that Hegseth committed specific war crimes remain allegations contained in the articles. No court has ruled on those claims. Hegseth’s characterization of the process as sabotage is his description of the political context, not a finding about the legal status of the underlying operations. Trump’s description of the vote as an exoneration is a political assessment of the procedural outcome.

Mechanism a reader needs

Impeachment of a cabinet officer begins in the House. A privileged resolution can force a vote, but the majority can use a motion to refer the matter to committee, which has the practical effect of delaying or ending consideration in the current Congress. The Judiciary Committee is currently chaired by Republicans. Removal from office would require a two-thirds vote in the Senate after a trial. Democrats are expected to hold the majority in both chambers beginning in January, so any future articles would face a different majority calculus.

The midterm elections the previous week produced Democratic control of the next House and Senate. Several Republican members had publicly linked electoral losses to the costs and duration of the Iran conflict. The procedural vote therefore occurred while the current majority still controlled the calendar and while the secretary retained the public support of the president.

The Pentagon briefing was Hegseth’s first on-camera appearance at the department in six months. Timing placed it hours before the House vote. The referral vote is recorded in the Congressional Record; the exact party-line breakdown and the list of absent members are public.

What this story is not

It is not a completed impeachment. It is not a Senate trial. It is not a judicial determination that any military operation was unlawful. The photograph is an aerial view of the Pentagon from 2023, not an image of the 10 November briefing or of any particular member of Congress.
