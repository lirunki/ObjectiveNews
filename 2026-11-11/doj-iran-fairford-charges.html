Washington / Gloucestershire · Security · 11 November 2026

Justice Department charges three men in alleged Iran-backed plots that include RAF Fairford.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

On 10 November the Justice Department unsealed an indictment charging Mohammad Baqer Saad Dawood Al Saadi (dual Iranian-Iraqi), Mohsen Mohajeri (Iranian), and Mohammad Ali Al Hassani (Iraqi) with terrorism-related offenses. Prosecutors state that the three operated on behalf of a network linked to Kataib Hezbollah and Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. The charges cover nearly twenty alleged attacks or attempted attacks in Europe and the United States, including a plot directed at RAF Fairford in Gloucestershire in September 2026. Fairford has hosted US bomber aircraft.

British authorities have publicly attributed the Fairford incident to Iran. The US and several European foreign ministries issued a joint statement earlier in the autumn condemning hostile Iranian activities. The indictment is a charging document; it contains the government’s allegations. The defendants have not been convicted.

What is only a quote

Descriptions of the network as directed by the IRGC are the prosecutors’ characterization. Statements by Iranian officials denying involvement are denials, not independent verification. Claims about the precise number of successful attacks versus foiled plots remain within the charging language until trial evidence is presented.

Mechanism a reader needs

US prosecutors can charge foreign nationals for acts that affect US persons or property, including plots against US forces stationed abroad. RAF Fairford is a Royal Air Force station made available to the United States for bomber operations. A plot against the base therefore falls within the jurisdictional theories used in the indictment. The September incident led to arrests in the United Kingdom and the subsequent withdrawal of US bombers from the base in early October.

The indictment expands earlier charges and consolidates multiple alleged operations. Trial would require extradition or presence of the defendants in a US court. Public reporting so far does not state the current custody status of all three men.

What this story is not

It is not a completed attack on Fairford. It is not a finding that Iran ordered every listed incident. The photograph shows B-1 Lancers at the base in 2023 during a routine deployment, not the 2026 security response.
