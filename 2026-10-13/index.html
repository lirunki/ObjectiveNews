El Supremo mantiene la suspensión del voto de los nacionalizados por la ley de nietos

El 12 de octubre la Sala de lo Contencioso-Administrativo del Tribunal Supremo desestimó el recurso de la Abogacía del Estado y mantuvo la medida cautelar de septiembre que suspende los efectos electorales de 163.020 personas nacionalizadas bajo la disposición adicional de la Ley de Memoria Democrática (“ley de nietos”) para las elecciones del 29 de noviembre. Solo quienes obtengan certificación consular de que sus antepasados sufrieron exilio por razones políticas, ideológicas o de orientación sexual conservan el derecho. Hubo un voto particular. Federal agents searched Rep. Ilhan Omar’s Washington home under a judicial warrant

On 12 October Homeland Security Investigations executed a search warrant obtained by the Justice Department at the Washington-area residence of Rep. Ilhan Omar (D-MN). The DOJ confirmed the warrant was judicially authorized and part of an ongoing criminal investigation; sources said it concerns immigration paperwork filled out when Omar was a child. No charges have been announced. Omar called it political intimidation. The search occurred three weeks before midterms. Trump’s Russian diesel arrangement draws bipartisan and European reaction

On 9 October President Trump said Russia would immediately supply more than 300,000 metric tons of diesel, with further volumes later, and the Treasury issued a temporary license lifting sanctions on Russian diesel exports until April. Kremlin statements confirmed willingness to supply oil products. Ukrainian and European officials criticized the step as undermining sanctions intended to limit Russian war revenues. Volumes announced are small relative to daily US diesel demand of roughly 2.9 million barrels. Neither side has independently verified an energy-infrastructure ceasefire. One person died in an incident on the Goliath roller coaster at Six Flags Over Georgia

On the afternoon of 12 October one person died and several others were injured at Six Flags Over Georgia. Cobb County police’s major crimes unit is investigating. Witnesses described a man falling from the Goliath coaster and striking the train before landing on a structure below; officials have not confirmed the mechanism. Six Flags temporarily closed nine similar rides at other parks “out of an abundance of caution.” The photograph is a 2006 type image of the ride structure, not the incident scene or the injured.
