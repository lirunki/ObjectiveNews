Washington · diplomacy / technology · 24–26 September 2026

After a three-day Washington visit, the United States and China said they would open a channel for artificial-intelligence incidents.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

Chinese President Xi Jinping completed a multi-day visit to Washington that included arrival ceremonies, closed talks with President Donald Trump, a state dinner and a Saturday departure reported by Xinhua. On Saturday both governments described an agreement to establish a communication mechanism for artificial-intelligence incidents and to keep working on military crisis communications.

U.S. and secondary accounts place a further AI-specific discussion around November. China’s Foreign Ministry readout said the sides could continue dialogue on AI risks and benefits and that AI should remain under human control. No public memorandum published by Saturday defined what counts as an AI incident, which agencies would staff a hotline, or how either side would verify the other’s report.

What is only a quote

Before the talks Trump wrote that Super Intelligence would be a topic but he wanted to leave it where it is, adding that the DOJ is the guardrail. He has called existential-risk warnings a hoax and said the United States would not put brakes on the technology. Xi said both countries have a responsibility to manage AI for good under human control. Commentary that the summit was pomp without substance, or historic friendship, is framing.

Mechanism and history a reader needs

Great-power military hotlines exist because accidents can escalate faster than cabinets meet. An AI-incident channel copies that logic onto software systems that can act at machine speed. Copying the logic is not the same as building the wiring. A notification channel does not freeze training runs, cap compute, or require third-party audits. Trade items announced in the same week travel on a different track.

What the story is not

This is not a verified arms-control treaty for models. It is not proof that either government has accepted the other’s risk theory. It is not evidence that super intelligence exists as an engineering object.
