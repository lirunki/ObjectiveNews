Nor’easter floods the Northeast coast; one death and more than 100,000 peak outages

The agreed facts are meteorological and municipal. The National Weather Service described a slow-moving coastal cyclone that would keep high winds, heavy rain and tidal flooding on the Mid-Atlantic and New England through Monday. PowerOutage.us and Reuters tracked a Saturday peak above 100,000 customers without electricity. NYPD and NYCHA said a fallen tree killed NYCHA employee Leighton Brown, 56, in Brooklyn. FlightAware counted more than 400 cancellations Saturday. FEMA said coordination centers were on an enhanced posture; that is not a disaster declaration.

Cientos de personas amanecen acampadas en Sol tras la marcha por la vivienda
