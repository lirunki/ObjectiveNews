Washington · press access · 25–26 September 2026

The White House left CNN off the Saturday Air Force One television pool for a Tennessee trip.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

CNN was scheduled to serve as the rotating U.S. network television pooler for President Trump’s 26 September trip to Knoxville, Tennessee. Friday-night White House guidance did not list CNN in that role. The Hill, the Boston Globe and Deadline reported that CNN journalists were not approved to board Air Force One. Some accounts said Real America’s Voice was named instead. The other pool participants declined to take CNN’s seat. A Saturday morning pool report said there was no TV pool.

On 24 September Judge Timothy J. Kelly issued a TRO directing the government to restore hard-pass credentials revoked on or about 18 September for employees of CNN, MS NOW and POLITICO. The TRO addresses White House complex access. It does not write a seating chart for Air Force One. Earlier on Friday CNN had performed some pool duties after the TRO. The Saturday omission is a new operational choice.

What is only a quote

Trump posted that fake news should not be allowed in the White House. That is a political accusation, not the text of Judge Kelly’s order. The White House Correspondents’ Association described the pool as the system that lets networks share video of the president. That is an accurate description of purpose and still an advocacy statement.

Mechanism and history a reader needs

The television pool exists because aircraft space is finite. Five broadcast organizations rotate camera duty and share the feed. Hard passes are a separate instrument from trip manifests. A TRO can put a reporter back on the driveway while the travel office still controls who is on the plane.

What the story is not

This is not a final First Amendment judgment. It is not proof that CNN’s reporting last week was false. It is not proof that the White House has fully defied Judge Kelly on hard passes.
