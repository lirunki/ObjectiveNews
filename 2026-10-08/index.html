Reported deaths in Pryluky rose from 13 to 22 as rescuers worked a five-storey block

Reuters, the BBC and the New Voice agree a Russian missile hit a five-storey block in Pryluky on 7 October and that children were among the dead. They do not agree a single number because the number moved. A Reuters item quoted Governor Chaus at 13 dead, four of them children. A later Reuters story quoted Zelenskyy at 19 in the town and 25 nationwide, and Interior Minister Vyhivskyi at 28 nationwide. The BBC’s update and an 8 October emergency-service figure used by the New Voice both say 22 in the block, five of them children, with about 50 to 54 injured. Russia’s claim of military-industrial targets is a quote. The birthday line is a presidential comment. The still is a 2015 Chernihiv park, not the rubble.

The Abraham Lincoln was due home after Hegseth read a Presidential Unit Citation

AP, Stars and Stripes and ABC agree the carrier was due at North Island on 8 October and that Hegseth presented a Presidential Unit Citation on the 7th for Iran-war operations. AP’s uninterrupted-at-sea figure is 265 days; Stars and Stripes’ deployment length is 321 days. Those are different clocks. Combat totals — 3,693 flights, 450 Tomahawks, 64 ships, 77 drones in one reading; “almost 4,000” sorties and 1.5 million pounds in another — are the citation speech, not an audit. The claim that the cruise stopped a nuclear weapon is a sentence, not a measurement. The still is the hull in dry dock in 1990.
