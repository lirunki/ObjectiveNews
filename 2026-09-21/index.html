ICE officer shoots Venezuelan man in Austin during foot pursuit

Austin Police Chief Lisa Davis said an ICE officer shot a man once in the torso shortly before 1 p.m. local time on 20 September after a brief foot pursuit that followed a vehicle stop under a highway overpass on the city’s north side. The man, identified by attorney Kate Lincoln-Goldfinch as 28-year-old Wilber Rafael Garces Perez from Venezuela, was taken to hospital in serious but stable condition and later released into federal custody. DHS stated he entered illegally under the prior administration and held a final removal order; the attorney initially said he had a work permit and entered legally, later acknowledging uncertainty on current status. Local officials noted they were not part of the operation and requested independent review. Protesters gathered at the scene. The shooting is the latest in a series of ICE use-of-force incidents under the intensified deportation campaign. Body-camera footage and exact threat assessment have not been released publicly.

Networks suspend pool coverage after White House bars CNN, MS NOW, Politico

On 21 September major television networks agreed to suspend their pooled coverage of President Trump after the White House barred CNN, MS NOW and Politico from the grounds. The three outlets said they would file suit the same day, arguing the ban violates the First Amendment. Trump had announced the ban on 18 September via Truth Social, calling the outlets “Fake News” and stating they constantly report fiction; reporters were denied entry on Saturday. CNN was removed from television pool duties on Monday. The White House press pool is a rotating group that covers the president for the broader corps. No court order has yet issued. Prior administrations have restricted individual reporters but mass outlet bans of this form are rare in recent decades.

Marlaska y Feijóo viajan a Ceuta en plena disputa por la gestión migratoria

El ministro del Interior Fernando Grande-Marlaska y el líder del PP Alberto Núñez Feijóo viajaron a Ceuta el 21 de septiembre en el contexto de la crisis migratoria que se arrastra desde el salto masivo de finales de julio. Las autoridades locales y el Gobierno central discrepan sobre el número residual de migrantes, el tratamiento de los menores no acompañados y el grado de colaboración de Marruecos. La Fiscalía mantiene abiertos expedientes de protección tras denuncias de abusos a menores. Manifestantes apedrearon el vehículo de una diputada local. El Gobierno sostiene que la mayoría de los menores quieren quedarse y la ley los protege; la oposición exige devoluciones y acusa de cronificación. Las cifras exactas de personas aún en la ciudad y de plazas disponibles en centros temporales siguen sin consolidarse públicamente.

El juez Peinado abre juicio oral a Begoña Gómez por tráfico de influencias y malversación

El juez Juan Carlos Peinado abrió juicio oral con jurado contra Begoña Gómez, esposa del presidente Pedro Sánchez, por los delitos de tráfico de influencias y malversación. El Gobierno condenó la decisión como «persecución judicial» y «arbitraria». La instrucción se ha centrado en contratos y relaciones con empresas que habrían obtenido beneficios presuntamente ligados a la posición de Gómez. La defensa niega los hechos. El caso se suma a otras investigaciones que afectan al entorno de Moncloa y mantiene la tensión política en el Congreso. La fecha del juicio con jurado no se ha fijado públicamente en el momento de este resumen.
