Santa Clara · Corporate · 12 November 2026

Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia President and CEO; Colette Kress appointed interim

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed

Nvidia Corporation stated on 12 November 2026 that its Board of Directors accepted the resignation of Jen-Hsun Huang (Jensen Huang) as President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board, effective immediately. Colette M. Kress, age 59, current Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, was appointed interim Chief Executive Officer. She will continue in her finance role while serving as interim CEO until a permanent successor is named. The Board is initiating a search considering internal and external candidates. Harvey C. Jones was appointed the first-ever Chair of the Board. These details appear in the company’s newsroom release and in the 8-K regulatory filing.

Shares of Nvidia fell sharply in early trading following the announcement, with reports of declines of around 12 percent at the open and partial recovery later. The company has scheduled an employee meeting and is due to report third-quarter fiscal results on 17 November.

What is only a quote or report

Multiple wire services and technology outlets reported that the resignation followed days of coverage concerning personal relationships involving Huang. One report concerned a relationship with a woman described by U.S. authorities as a suspected Chinese spy; another, published by The New York Times, concerned a relationship with a company executive who reported to him. Huang was reported to have apologized in some accounts. These details are attributed to reporting and sources; the official company statement does not confirm or elaborate on them. No independent public record of criminal charges or completed internal investigation findings has been released at the time of writing.

Mechanism and context

Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 and led it for 33 years, during which the company became central to graphics processing units and, more recently, to accelerated computing for artificial intelligence. Nvidia’s market capitalization had reached several trillion dollars. Leadership transitions at such firms affect customer roadmaps, investor confidence and regulatory discussions concerning chip exports. The appointment of a long-serving CFO as interim provides continuity in financial reporting while a permanent search proceeds. Naming a board chair for the first time alters governance structure.

What the story is not

This is not a finding that any national-security investigation has concluded, nor is it a statement that product roadmaps have changed. Market reactions are observed prices, not predictions. Personal matters remain reported claims until confirmed by primary sources.
