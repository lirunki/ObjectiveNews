Iraq · U.S. military presence · 29 September 2026

U.S. forces are set to complete withdrawal from remaining Iraq bases by 30 September

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

U.S. and Iraqi officials state that the remaining coalition forces will leave their last bases in Iraq by 30 September 2026. The timetable originates in a September 2024 agreement between the United States and Iraq that ended the formal military mission of Operation Inherent Resolve inside federal Iraq. The agreement was reached under President Biden and has been carried out under President Trump. Public statements from the Pentagon and the State Department reaffirm the mutually agreed timeline. Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi has repeatedly cited 30 September as the terminal date. Reporting from Reuters, AP, and Stars and Stripes is consistent on the calendar.

Approximately 4,500 U.S. service members died in Iraq-related operations after the 2003 invasion. The last U.S. fatality linked to Iraq operations during the current conflict with Iran occurred in July 2026 near Erbil.

What is only a quote or assessment

Iran-aligned armed groups describe the departure as a historic victory. Some Iraqi security officials and a Kurdish commander have warned of Islamic State cell activity and reduced intelligence support. These are assessments, not completed post-withdrawal audits.

Mechanism and history a reader needs

U.S. forces entered Iraq in 2003 and withdrew at the end of 2011. They returned in 2014 to help defeat Islamic State. By 2024 both capitals preferred a formal end to the coalition combat mission while preserving bilateral cooperation. The 2024 agreement set a phased transition. The current Iran war has not altered the September 30 date according to official statements.

What the story is not

This is not a sudden unilateral exit. It is the terminal phase of a multi-year drawdown. Residual advisory cooperation is expected from outside Iraq. Claims of automatic security outcomes remain untested forecasts.
