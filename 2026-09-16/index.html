News helicopter crashes in Chatsworth while covering bus collision; three dead

A Eurocopter AS350 B2 operated for NBC Los Angeles and Telemundo 52 went down between commercial buildings in Chatsworth just before 19:00 local time on 15 September while covering a fatal Metro bus–SUV collision. Los Angeles Fire Department reported three dead (pilot, aerial journalist, and one pedestrian) and at least one hospitalized. NTSB and FAA will investigate. Agreed facts from LAFD statements and station confirmation; cause not yet determined. The still is a type photograph, not the accident aircraft.

Federal Reserve expected to raise rates for first time since 2023

Markets priced a greater than 90 percent probability of a 25-basis-point increase in the federal funds target range to 3.75–4.00 percent at the 15–16 September FOMC meeting, the first hike since July 2023. Chair Kevin Warsh and recent inflation data above the 2 percent target, together with energy-price effects from the Iran conflict, form the public rationale. The decision and Summary of Economic Projections are scheduled for 14:00 ET; any actual vote and statement language remain pending at the time of this dossier.

CBO estimates Iran war cost $38 billion through 1 August

The Congressional Budget Office reported that Department of Defense costs for combat operations against Iran totaled approximately $38 billion through 1 August 2026, with projected ongoing monthly costs of $2–3 billion depending on intensity. The largest component is munitions replacement ($21.7 billion). The estimate relies on public data because the Pentagon did not respond to CBO information requests; it excludes base-repair and personnel long-term costs. The figure is close to earlier public testimony by Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth.

Supreme Court leaves block on USPS mail-ballot rule in place for midterms

In an unsigned order the Supreme Court denied the administration’s emergency application to stay a district-court injunction that prevents the Postal Service from implementing portions of a new rule on mail-in ballots before the November midterms. Justice Kavanaugh concurred, noting insufficient time for election officials to implement changes; Justices Alito and Thomas dissented. Existing state procedures therefore remain operative for the 2026 cycle. The order does not decide the ultimate merits of the rule’s legality.

Crisis migratoria en Ceuta: declaraciones de Sánchez, controles fronterizos y detenciones

El primer ministro Pedro Sánchez insistió en el Congreso en que no hubo avisos previos de la magnitud del cruce masivo hacia Ceuta. Italia y España prorrogaron controles fronterizos recíprocos. La Guardia Civil detuvo a propietarios de un piso donde vivían 25 personas hacinadas, seis de ellas menores. Ursula von der Leyen reiteró que Ceuta es España y Europa. Los hechos acordados proceden de declaraciones oficiales y partes policiales; la escala de cualquier coordinación previa con Marruecos sigue en disputa política.
