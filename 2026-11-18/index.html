Mary Peltola wins Alaska Senate seat, flipping it for Democrats

Mary Peltola, a Democrat and former House member, defeated two-term Republican Sen. Dan Sullivan in Alaska. Multiple outlets projected the result on 17–18 November, describing it as giving Democrats a 56-44 Senate majority. Peltola is the first Alaska Native elected to the Senate. The race occurred under Alaska’s ranked-choice system. Allegations that a similarly named candidate was recruited to split the vote remain under investigation by state officials; Peltola’s campaign has denied involvement. The outcome is checkable against certified results once available. It does not by itself determine legislative outcomes in the next Congress.

Apple services and NSA network report outages amid broader tech disruptions

Apple confirmed widespread outages affecting Apple Pay, iCloud, and other services on 17 November. Separately, the NSA reported a temporary degradation of its classified internal network (NSANet), with some staff unable to access systems. Officials have stated there is no evidence of a cyberattack so far. Other outages earlier in the week affected airlines, banks, and government systems. The pattern is checkable against company status pages and agency statements. It is not evidence of a single coordinated cause.

Huelga ferroviaria de 24 horas paraliza servicios de Renfe y AVE

El 17 de noviembre los sindicatos convocaron una huelga general de 24 horas en el sector ferroviario español. Renfe informó de cancelaciones masivas en Cercanías de Madrid (hasta el 97 % en algunos tramos) y afectaciones en alta velocidad. El Ministerio de Transportes fijó servicios mínimos. La huelga coincidió con anuncios de subida salarial para funcionarios. Los porcentajes de seguimiento y cancelaciones son cifras de la operadora y son verificables en comunicados oficiales. No constituye un cierre total del sistema.
