Trump, Xi and Putin issue four-point peace declaration after Shenzhen meeting

The three leaders met for about 90 minutes at Kylin Villa on the sidelines of APEC. China and Russia published a joint declaration that reaffirms “a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought,” calls for UN reform, greater refined-petroleum supplies, and respect for official immunities against ICC-style jurisdiction. The text does not mention Ukraine or the Iran conflict by name. Trump left without a public statement. The existence and wording of the declaration are checkable against Xinhua and Kremlin releases. Claims of “breakthrough” or “historic peace” are framing, not independent verification of new enforcement mechanisms.

Anthropic prices IPO at $83, raises record $81.3 billion

Anthropic set the price at $83 on 18 November, above its $70–$78 range, and sold 980 million shares for $81.3 billion. A parallel $26 billion private placement brought total capital raised to $107.3 billion and a fully diluted valuation near $1.5 trillion. Shares were scheduled to begin trading on Nasdaq under ANT on 19 November. The figures come from company filings and wire reports. Market commentary about an “AI bubble” is opinion; the pricing and share count are checkable against the prospectus.

House Democrats nominate Hakeem Jeffries for speaker 251-11

On 18 November the House Democratic Caucus elected Jeffries by secret ballot 251-11. The result puts him on track to become the first Black speaker when the new Congress convenes in January, provided he receives a majority of the full House. Eleven progressive members voted against the nomination; they issued a statement listing remaining policy demands. The vote tally is reported consistently by Reuters, NPR and CNN. The floor election remains prospective.

Gobierno y sindicatos desconvocan la huelga de controladores del 19 de noviembre

El vicepresidente Carlos Cuerpo anunció un acuerdo con CCOO, UGT y SCF que permitió desconvocar el paro de controladores de Adif previsto para el 19 de noviembre. El pacto incluye un estudio de plantillas en un mes, doble verificación del tráfico, formación revisada y publicación diaria de limitaciones de velocidad. Semaf, el sindicato de maquinistas, no firmó y mantiene su convocatoria para el 23 de noviembre. Las desconvocatorias y los puntos del acuerdo son verificables en comunicados del Ministerio de Transportes y de los sindicatos firmantes.
