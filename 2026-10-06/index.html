Francis Halzen won the physics Nobel for IceCube

The Academy’s citation is the decision: decisive contributions to IceCube and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. Reuters, the BBC and the press release agree on Halzen, Wisconsin–Madison, a cubic kilometre of South Pole ice, completion in 2011, and 12 million kronor undivided. The instrument times rare flashes when a neutrino hits a nucleus. Halzen’s “lucky” and “surprise” lines are a phone call from Italy, not a new event count. The Commons frame is the surface station in January 2023, not Stockholm.

Validation now sits with the Permanent Deputation, where the PNV is described as the swing vote, not as a vote already cast. Sánchez said the open-ended rent-extension decree still lacks the numbers. The Pentagon said Trump approved a firing-squad method in the Hasan case

The Monday fact is an approval announcement, not an execution. Spokesman Sean Parnell said Secretary Hegseth recommended, and Trump approved, a firing squad for Nidal Hasan, convicted in 2013 of the 2009 Fort Hood killings. The toll the wires repeat is 13 dead and 32 wounded. The Army secretary is to set time and place; none is published. “First military execution since 1961” is conditional on the sentence being carried out. Fox’s “terrorist” is a label; the conviction described is premeditated murder under the UCMJ. The still is the Pentagon in 2008.

Treasury warned foreign banks of sanctions without notice

Monday’s OFAC alert is a warning, not a new designation list. Reuters reports the line that foreign banks still dealing with Iran or its financial sector may be sanctioned without advance notice, under the label Operation Economic Outcast. The alert points back to an August announcement and a 4 September designation of Golden Global Bank. Correspondent-account access is the lever described. No piece counts banks that exited overnight.
