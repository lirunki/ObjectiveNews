Virginia and DC sue to halt proposed 250-foot arch near Memorial Bridge

The attorneys general of DC and Virginia filed suit on 31 October arguing the proposed arch lacks congressional authorization, violates height and environmental-review statutes, and would cause concrete traffic and environmental harm. AP and NPR report the site is near the western end of Arlington Memorial Bridge and that the administration has said work could start as early as the following week. Judge Chutkan consolidated the case with an earlier veterans’ suit and set a briefing schedule; no ruling on a temporary halt has issued. The claims of “flagrantly unlawful” or of national-security necessity are party arguments, not findings. The existing bridge photograph is a type still, not a picture of any construction.

56 de 69 fallecidos identificados en el choque de Urda; 31 hospitalizados

El CID y las agencias coinciden en que 56 de las 69 víctimas mortales del choque del 28 de octubre en Urda ya están identificadas (54 españolas, una francesa, una portuguesa), 52 por huella y 4 por ADN. Quedan 13 pendientes de cooperación internacional. Los hospitalizados en Castilla-La Mancha han bajado a 31, 13 de ellos en UCI. La jueza pide acelerar la retirada de vagones. Las afirmaciones sobre la cadena exacta de fallos de los sistemas de protección son aún de investigación, no el informe final. La imagen es una fotografía de tipo de un ETR 1000, no los restos del accidente.

Judge dismisses with prejudice Trump’s $10 billion suit against the Wall Street Journal

Judge Darrin Gayles ruled on 31 October that the amended complaint failed to allege actual malice, the standard required for a public-figure defamation claim. The dismissal is with prejudice. Reuters and BBC report the suit concerned the Journal’s 2025 article on a letter in a 2003 Epstein birthday album; Trump has denied authorship. The court did not decide authenticity or truth. Party statements about the story being “malicious” or about further litigation are not findings. The image is an unrelated neutral still.
