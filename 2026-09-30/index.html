White House and AI executives sign a voluntary four-layer review accord

The released page asks for internal monitors, an internal check team, an outside auditor, and a board committee. Signatures include Trump, Amodei, Pichai, Zuckerberg, Brockman, Huang, and Musk on the lists that match the White House/Truth Social block. “Morally binding” and “almost like a Constitution” are the president’s labels. There is no statute, no penalty, and no required publication of failed tests. Score the quotes as quotes. The document is a political preference for self-regulation after a week of contested agent-misbehavior reports.
