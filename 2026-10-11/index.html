Erie police: eight killed including children and a pregnant woman, plus the suspect, at a Perry Street home

Erie Police Chief Rick Lorah said a 45-year-old man related to seven of the victims killed eight people Friday afternoon at a home on Perry Street, including a 5-year-old boy, a roughly 22-month-old girl, and a woman who was about eight months pregnant, before taking his own life. Two women were found dead outside; the rest inside. All deaths occurred before officers arrived, according to the chief. No names have been released. Police described the scene as horrific and noted mental health as a factor under investigation. The still is the Bicentennial Tower in Erie, a location photograph, not the Perry Street house.
