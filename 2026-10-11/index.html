Erie police: eight killed including children and a pregnant woman, plus the suspect, at a Perry Street home

Erie Police Chief Rick Lorah said a 45-year-old man related to seven of the victims killed eight people Friday afternoon at a home on Perry Street, including a 5-year-old boy, a roughly 22-month-old girl, and a woman who was about eight months pregnant, before taking his own life. Two women were found dead outside; the rest inside. All deaths occurred before officers arrived, according to the chief. No names have been released. Police described the scene as horrific and noted mental health as a factor under investigation. The still is the Bicentennial Tower in Erie, a location photograph, not the Perry Street house.

Saudi authorities: 12 killed, more than 300 wounded in attack on Riyadh airport

The Saudi General Authority of Civil Aviation stated that a Saturday attack on King Khalid International Airport killed 12 people and wounded 309. The dead included nationals from Saudi Arabia, Bangladesh, the United States, Egypt, Jordan, Syria, Palestine, and Sudan. Operations were suspended. A Saudi-led coalition blamed Iran-backed Houthi forces. This was the third reported attack on the airport that week. President Trump said he was considering further U.S. action against the Houthis. The photograph is a pre-event exterior of the airport, not the damaged terminal.

Trump: time for Ukraine to get a new president, citing diesel prices and Russia deal

On Saturday President Trump told reporters that Ukraine should get a new president who can make a deal, and that President Zelenskyy had rejected opportunities to end the war. He linked the comments to high U.S. diesel prices and a Friday announcement of a temporary diesel-supply arrangement with Russia. Zelenskyy had criticized the arrangement. Ukraine remains under martial law, suspending ordinary elections. The photograph is the White House exterior, not the press interaction.

Justice Department investigating five TV networks over White House pool coverage halt

A Justice Department spokesperson confirmed that the Antitrust Division is investigating whether ABC, CBS, CNN, NBC, and Fox News violated antitrust law by temporarily suspending White House television pool coverage after the administration revoked credentials for three outlets in September. A federal judge later issued a temporary order restoring access for those outlets. The department framed the halt as a possible group boycott under the Sherman Act. No charges have been filed. The photograph is the Justice Department headquarters exterior.

Policía Nacional completa el desalojo del asentamiento de El Guano en Ceuta

La Policía Nacional inició este domingo el desalojo del último gran asentamiento de migrantes en la explanada de El Guano, en Ceuta, a poco más de 48 horas de la visita de los Reyes. Adultos fueron trasladados al CATE o al entorno fronterizo; menores, a dependencias policiales para identificación. Las estimaciones de ocupantes varían entre unos 150 y más de 500. El operativo sigue a desalojos previos en otras zonas de la ciudad. La fotografía es una vista aérea de Ceuta, no el momento del desalojo.

Sánchez en acto de precampaña en Ponferrada; Rufián encabezará la lista de ERC

El presidente del Gobierno, Pedro Sánchez, participó este domingo en un acto de precampaña en la Térmica Cultural de Ponferrada, el segundo tras el de Dos Hermanas. Llamó a la movilización para el 29 de noviembre. Por separado, ERC confirmó que Gabriel Rufián encabezará su lista al Congreso. Las listas definitivas se aprobarán el 20 de octubre. La fotografía es el complejo de la Moncloa, no el recinto del acto.
