Judge blocks immediate closure of East Potomac Park for Trump golf renovation

U.S. District Judge Ana Reyes on Saturday ordered that East Potomac Park remain open for now, blocking a planned Sunday closure for renovations that President Trump has described as creating a championship-level course. The order, effective through 28 November, does not require the National Park Service to continue golf operations after the operator’s lease was terminated. Reyes found the government’s stated safety justifications pretextual and said the administration violated procedural requirements. Iran claims drone strikes on Bahrain airbase; Bahrain says attacks intercepted

SpaceX catches Starship upper stage for the first time after orbital flight

On Saturday, SpaceX’s Starship upper stage returned from a 22-hour mission that included 14 Earth orbits and deployment of 26 Starlink satellites, and was caught by the mechanical arms of the launch tower at Starbase, Texas. The booster stage had been diverted to a splashdown after an early abort of its boostback burn. This was the first recovery of the ship itself. SpaceX described the catch as a milestone for full reusability. The booster failure remains under investigation. The photograph is a type image of the tower from earlier testing, not Saturday’s flight.

FAA ground-stop recovery linked to L3Harris router during fiber upgrade

Officials said Saturday that the nearly five-hour nationwide ground stop on Friday, which disrupted more than 20,000 flights, was traced to a router installed by L3Harris during routine replacement of copper lines with fiber at a remote FAA site. The new equipment flooded the network with connection attempts, affecting multiple en-route centers. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy stated there was no evidence of a cyberattack. The FAA has disabled similar functions on other routers. Full recovery of schedules is ongoing. The photograph is a type image of an ATC tower exterior.

Récord de rescates en Canarias: cerca de 1.500 migrantes en 24 horas en El Hierro

El sábado llegaron a El Hierro 14 cayucos con 1.488 inmigrantes, la cifra más alta en un solo día en los 32 años de la Ruta Canaria. El ministro de Política Territorial, Ángel Víctor Torres, visitó la isla. Las autoridades y ONGs reportan presión sobre los servicios de acogida. Los números proceden de recuentos oficiales y de Salvamento Marítimo; las causas del aumento son objeto de debate entre factores de origen y políticas de frontera.
