Five UK nationals arrested near RAF Fairford on explosives and terrorism suspicion

Police received a call at 00:45 BST Sunday about three suspicious vehicles near the base. Five London men aged 23–25 were arrested under the Explosives Act and later on suspicion of preparing a terrorist act. An Army disposal team examined the vans; ~85 homes were evacuated. Trump said the suspects sought “big damage” and had been under view; police have not publicly attributed motive. The Iranian embassy denied involvement. The base has hosted U.S. bombers used in Iran operations.

Nor’easter floods the Northeast coast; peak outages above 100,000 and one death
