Florida · crime · 26 October 2026

Tallahassee police continue investigation after 11 people were shot near Florida A&M University’s stadium.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

Tallahassee Police Chief Lawrence Revell and subsequent departmental statements said the shooting occurred just before 8 p.m. local time on Saturday, 24 October 2026, near the intersection of Perry and Manatee streets, on the edge of the Florida A&M University campus next to Bragg Memorial Stadium. The university had hosted its homecoming football game earlier that evening against the University of Arkansas-Pine Bluff; the visitors won 31-28. Homecoming week activities had drawn alumni and families to the area.

Police reported that 11 people sustained gunshot wounds. A twelfth person, identified as the grandmother of one of the victims, was injured while fleeing the scene. Of the 12 injured, six were adults and six were juveniles. The youngest victim was 11 years old. At least three victims suffered serious injuries; one remained in critical condition into Sunday. By Sunday morning, five victims had been released from the hospital and six remained hospitalized. No deaths were reported.

Chief Revell said preliminary information indicated that a physical altercation involving two or three individuals preceded the gunfire. He described the incident as isolated. No arrests had been announced as of the latest reports available on Monday morning, 26 October. The department asked the public for tips and established channels for information and video. Florida A&M University stated that the shooting occurred near campus, that the campus itself remained safe, and that it shared the community’s outrage and sadness.

What is only a quote

Statements that the shooting “stemmed from a fight” or was “an isolated incident between two or three individuals” are preliminary police characterizations. They have not been tested in court or confirmed by video that has been released publicly. Descriptions of motive remain absent from official statements. Expressions of heartbreak by the chief or university president are personal or institutional responses; they do not establish facts about who fired or why.

Mechanism a reader needs

Florida A&M University is a historically Black public land-grant university in Tallahassee with roughly 10,000 students. Bragg Memorial Stadium is its football venue. Homecoming weekends concentrate large numbers of students, alumni, and visitors in streets adjacent to campus. Crowded outdoor areas after events can place bystanders near disputes that escalate. Police response times, hospital capacity, and the distinction between “near campus” and “on campus” matter for both operational reporting and community safety messaging. The university’s statement that campus remained safe is a security assessment; it does not address conditions on surrounding public streets.

Mass-casualty response protocols in U.S. cities typically include scene security, triage, transport to trauma centers, and later investigation by a violent-crimes unit. The absence of immediate arrests is common when shooters leave the scene and witnesses are dispersed. Tip lines and public video submission are standard tools. Until forensic results, witness interviews, and any recovered video are completed, the public record consists of the police timeline and injury counts.

General-aviation or campus-event shootings often generate rapid political commentary. The checkable elements here are the location, the time window, the injury numbers given by police, the age breakdown of victims, the hospital status updates, and the fact that no arrests had been announced by early Monday. Claims about broader causes or patterns require separate evidence.

It is not a confirmed connection between the football game result and the shooting. It is not a finding that the university campus was unsafe. It is not an identification of suspects. The photograph above is a type image of a campus sign; it is not a picture of the intersection on Saturday night.
