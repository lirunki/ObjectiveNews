DOJ sues to block Alaska Senate certification if Sullivan loses

The Department of Justice filed a complaint on 22 October in the District of Alaska asking a federal judge to prevent certification of the 3 November Senate result under a specific condition: if incumbent Dan S. Sullivan does not win and if treating every vote for Daniel J. Sullivan as a vote for the senator would have produced a ranked-choice win. President Trump said he directed the filing. The complaint treats the second candidacy as intended to confuse voters; the candidate has denied that purpose. Alaska’s ranked-choice system redistributes ballots of eliminated candidates. The requested remedy is a counterfactual reallocation, not the count the state will perform. Early voting is under way. No court has yet ruled. The suit is an administrative filing, not evidence that votes have already been miscast.

White House says Melania Trump secured Oleshky ceasefire for aid and evacuation

The White House stated on 23 October that First Lady Melania Trump secured a localized ceasefire in Russian-occupied Oleshky so that humanitarian aid could enter and civilians could leave. The White House gave a start date of 29 October; Ukrainian officials said 27 October. Oleshky’s remaining population is estimated at 1,700–2,000, mostly elderly, down from about 24,000 before the war, with roughly 90 percent of the city flooded after the Kakhovka Dam collapse. Both sides confirmed an intention to pause military action in the city and necessary corridors. Implementation details have not been published. The arrangement is described as temporary and limited; it does not alter territorial control. Whether the pause holds and whether residents are moved will be observable after the announced start.

Feijóo reitera disposición a gobernar con Vox ante las elecciones del 29-N

Pedro Sánchez disolvió las Cortes el 5 de octubre y convocó elecciones generales para el 29 de noviembre tras la derrota de decretos de vivienda. Las encuestas desde entonces sitúan al PP por delante y proyectan mayoría absoluta para PP más Vox. El 23 de octubre Feijóo dijo en Cadena SER que no descartaba gobernar con Vox y que revisaría las leyes de aborto y eutanasia. También prometió elevar la construcción de viviendas a 200.000 unidades anuales. No hay acuerdo formal de coalición publicado. La campaña oficial va del 13 al 27 de noviembre. El resultado del 29 de noviembre determinará si se forma una mayoría de 176 escaños o se abren negociaciones adicionales. Las promesas programáticas no son hechos consumados.
