FAA recovery continues; Duffy seeks $50 billion for air-traffic modernization

The FAA issued a nationwide ground stop on 13 November after a telecommunications failure linked by officials to a router installed by contractor L3Harris during routine copper-to-fiber work at a remote site. Approximately 12,000 flights were canceled that day and several thousand more over the weekend. By Monday morning cancellations had fallen to a few hundred. Duffy has described the system as relying on decades-old equipment and has asked Congress for emergency funding on the order of $50 billion. The official FAA Q&A states that safety was never compromised and that all aircraft in the air landed safely. The photograph is a type image of an ATC tower exterior, not a picture of the outage or any specific facility involved in the upgrade.

China confirms first Xi-Trump-Putin meeting for Thursday in Shenzhen

China’s Foreign Ministry spokesperson Mao Ning said the Thursday meeting of the three leaders on the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen is of groundbreaking significance as the first face-to-face gathering of the heads of state of China, Russia, and the United States. The White House has stated that Trump will raise nuclear-arms reductions and issues related to the Iran conflict; officials have said they do not expect an agreement and have downplayed discussion of Ukraine. Putin originally proposed the trilateral format. No public agenda has been fixed. The photograph is a type exterior of the White House, not a picture of any of the leaders or of Shenzhen.

Encuestas del 29N: PP y Vox mantienen la mayoría absoluta a 13 días

El promedio de sondeos de elDiario.es y otras fuentes sitúa la suma de PP y Vox en torno a 193 escaños, por encima de la mayoría absoluta de 176. El PP ronda el 31 % y el PSOE el 27 %. Las encuestas privadas coinciden en la ventaja del bloque de la derecha; el CIS oficial ha mostrado resultados distintos en el pasado. Quedan 13 días de campaña. Los resultados definitivos solo se conocerán el 29 de noviembre. La fotografía es del exterior del Congreso, un still de localización.

Yolanda Díaz anuncia subida del 5,3 % del SMI para 2027

La vicepresidenta segunda y ministra de Trabajo, Yolanda Díaz, anunció en Al Rojo Vivo que el Gobierno aprobará este martes una subida del salario mínimo interprofesional del 5,3 % para 2027, hasta 1.286 euros brutos en 14 pagas. La medida forma parte del discurso de campaña del bloque de izquierdas a 13 días de las elecciones. El anuncio es una declaración política; la aprobación formal y el texto legal aún no se han publicado. La fotografía es de tipo del Congreso.
