September payrolls rose 29,000; unemployment was 4.2 percent

BLS said both the payroll change and the unemployment rate changed little in September. The rate, 4.2 percent, and 7.1 million unemployed people sit inside a 4.1-to-4.3 band the agency says has held since March. The gain is below the prior twelve-month average of 45,000 and below the 90,000 median forecast Reuters cited. Health care added 17,000, slower than its recent pace, with nursing and residential care down 9,000. Hassett’s line that the print was not disappointing is a quote. CME odds of an October rate hike, 18 percent in one AP market item and under 20 percent in Reuters, are trader prices, not a Federal Reserve vote. August’s revision is directionally down in Reuters and numbered 133,000 for August in an AP item that does not say whether that figure is pre- or post-revision.

The Supreme Court granted review of detention without a bond hearing

Thursday’s order accepts a case on whether immigrants living in the United States may be held without a bond hearing under the administration’s reading of a 1996 law. AP names the vehicle: Ricardo Aparecido Barbosa da Cunha, a Brazilian citizen authorities say entered illegally about twenty years ago, applied for asylum in 2016, and was arrested in September 2025. Nine circuits have rejected mandatory detention without a bond hearing; the Fifth and Eighth have not. The Times adds a newsroom count of more than 500 district judges against the rule, and about 70,000 people in immigration detention versus about 40,000 before January 2025. The 70,000 is a stock figure, not a count of people held only under the new reading. A grant of review is not a merits holding. The third-country removal case is a separate, faster docket.

About 9,000 troops and a third carrier were reported heading to the Middle East

AP, citing one U.S. official, said roughly 9,000 troops — more than 7,000 sailors and 2,000 Marines — are en route with the Theodore Roosevelt strike group and the Makin Island amphibious group. The official said three carriers could be in the region by the end of October, joining George H.W. Bush and George Washington. The resulting naval presence was described as more than 20,000 sailors and Marines. That is a projection if the new group arrives, not a count already on station. Trump’s line that Iran will be hit “very hard” if a cockpit incident is tied to Tehran is a conditional threat, not a published finding that Iran directed the copilot. The Commons still is a June exercise arrival in Hawaii, not this transit.

El Congreso rechazó los dos decretos de vivienda

The Friday validation votes failed. The first decree, published to take effect at midnight Thursday, drew 172 yes and 178 no, with PP, Vox, Junts, and UPN against. The second, on ordinary-residence leases, drew 166 yes and 184 no in the LA NACION count. Failure to validate ends a royal decree-law; it does not require a separate repeal, which is why Diario de Navarra put the first text’s life at a little over twenty-four hours. Sánchez’s line that history will judge the no votes is a speech, not a legal effect. A 29 November snap election is cabinet speculation reported by LA NACION via elDiario.es, not a dissolution decree. The Commons frame is the Congress exterior, not the floor vote and not the 23 September eviction.
