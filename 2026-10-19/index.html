National Guard member shot while intervening in a fight in Southeast Washington

Interim Chief Jeffery Carroll stated that Guard members in a van exited to interrupt a fight around 11:10 p.m. on 18 October in the 400 block of 8th Street SE. An individual produced a firearm, was ordered to drop it, pointed it toward the Guard, and was shot in the abdomen after return fire; one Guard member was hit in the leg. Both injuries were non-life-threatening. AP, ABC, CBS and local reporting match on the time, location, injury sites and the chief’s sequence. The account is the initial official narrative, not a completed investigation. The image is a type photograph of Guard personnel in an earlier support role, not the incident.

Trump announced East Potomac Golf Links will close 15 November for a 15-month redesign

Trump posted that Interior and Tom Fazio had a plan for a championship course that would close the public facility on 15 November and reopen in about 15 months, remaining open to the public and capable of hosting majors. A federal judge had halted further tree removal after hundreds of trees were cut over a holiday weekend and had required notice of long-term plans. AP, NYT and Washington Post report the post and the litigation. The characterizations of the existing course and the major-hosting claim are the president’s statements, not independent findings or organizer commitments. Supreme Court declined to revive 2024 plea agreements for KSM and co-defendants

The Court denied certiorari on appeals by Khalid Sheikh Mohammed, Mustafa al-Hawsawi and Walid bin Attash seeking to reinstate 2024 plea agreements that would have removed the death penalty. Then-Defense Secretary Austin had revoked the deals; the D.C. Circuit upheld his authority. Reuters, CBS and CNN match on the denial and the procedural history. The order leaves the lower-court ruling in place but is not a merits decision. The image is a type exterior of the Supreme Court Building, not related to Guantánamo.

Musk posted he will give one SpaceX share to every U.S. child next year
