Federal judge ordered halt to remaining taxpayer-funded Trump television ads

On Sunday night Judge Jennifer Rochon of the Southern District of New York issued a two-week temporary restraining order requiring the administration to cancel any remaining airings of television ads paid for with taxpayer money that promote President Trump. The ads had drawn approximately $19–20 million from DHS funds. The Justice Department had said the campaign was ending that night; the judge found the case was not moot. Plaintiffs including Common Cause argued the spending violated restrictions on using appropriated funds for publicity or propaganda.
