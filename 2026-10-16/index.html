16-year-old kills three children and a teacher at Bend, Oregon elementary school

Bend Police Chief Mike Krantz said a 16-year-old local resident forced entry into Silver Rail Elementary by shooting through locked doors around 1 p.m. local time on 15 October, killing children aged 5, 8 and 11 plus one teacher. A community member pursued the shooter inside and engaged him; the shooter was killed. Two other people (a 7-year-old and a 54-year-old teacher) were critically injured. Schools in the district closed Friday. These details match across Reuters, BBC and local briefings. Trump cancels livestream of Nidal Hasan firing-squad execution

President Trump wrote on 15 October that the December 3 firing-squad execution of Nidal Hasan at Fort Hood will proceed but will not be livestreamed, after criticism including from Vice President Vance. Hasan was convicted of the 2009 shooting that killed 13 and wounded 32. The Pentagon had announced the livestream the previous week; Trump’s post reversed that plan while affirming the death sentence. Families of victims may attend in person. FBI and Postal Inspection Service investigate suspicious letters to election offices and lawmakers

On 15 October the FBI and US Postal Inspection Service said they were investigating suspicious mailings, some containing an unknown white powder, sent to at least five Democratic Senate offices and several state election offices. Many were intercepted before delivery. Officials described them as potentially hazardous and noted the timing three weeks before Election Day. No confirmed injuries or confirmed toxic agent have been reported in the initial statements. The image is a type photograph of the Capitol exterior, not an opened letter or a specific office.

Feijóo pide el despliegue de la Armada en Ceuta por incursiones marroquíes

El 16 de octubre Alberto Núñez Feijóo reclamó en X que Sánchez despliegue la Armada en Ceuta, convoque a la embajadora de Marruecos y llame a consultas al embajador español en Rabat, calificando las entradas de buques marroquíes como “agresión reiterada” y no un “hecho puntual”. El Gobierno negó que a la Guardia Civil o a la Armada les consten incidentes después del 30-31 de julio. La información de ABC sobre una patrullera el 15 de octubre se cita como fuente periodística.
