Eighth Circuit lifts stay on Iowa lead azide detonation

The checkable act is the Eighth Circuit’s order lifting the administrative stay on the EPA emergency permit for in-place detonation of degraded lead azide at the Middletown plant. The panel cited safeguards already in the permit and the greater risk of an uncontrolled explosion. Trump’s social-media statements deferred the decision to the court and the incoming governor-elect; the Army subsequently confirmed it would proceed on Wednesday under the restored permit. Environmental groups’ petition remains pending for full review, but the stay itself is gone. The amount, location, and scheduled date are consistent across wire and local reporting. No explosion has yet been reported as of this summary.

Nvidia board reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO

Nvidia’s own statement records the unanimous board vote to restore Huang to the CEO role after an eleven-day absence. The stock closed 31.5 percent higher. Trump’s CNBC comments claim he convinced the board and provided national-security assurances; the company cited government input without releasing details. The resignation followed published reports of personal conduct and an intelligence allegation; the board’s earlier public position against reinstatement is also on the record. Market capitalization recovery is arithmetic from the close; personal financial impact on disclosed holdings is an estimate from prior filings, not a new disclosure.

House Ethics finds sexual misconduct evidence; Rep. Gomez resigns

The checkable act is the House Ethics Committee’s release of a report finding substantial evidence that Rep. Jimmy Gomez made unwelcome sexual contact with a House staffer, and Gomez’s same-day announcement of resignation effective 1 December. The staffer’s account of two 2024 incidents is summarized in the report; the committee found it credible that the conduct was unwelcome. Gomez lost his reelection earlier in November. He disputes the completeness of the report while accepting responsibility for personal mistakes. No criminal charges have been filed. The resignation ends further House discipline because the member is leaving the chamber.
