Philadelphia TRACON outage produces multi-hour Northeast ground stops

A primary data circuit serving Philadelphia TRACON failed around 09:45 ET on 21 September; the backup fiber had already been severed by construction activity, according to FAA and NJ Transit statements. Ground stops affected Newark, JFK, LaGuardia, Philadelphia, Teterboro and briefly Boston. FlightAware aggregates circulated by Reuters, CNN and AP put the national impact near 7,000 delayed or canceled flights. Transportation Secretary Duffy announced late afternoon that lines were repaired and operations were resuming.
