Day summary — 5 November 2026

Prev day: 4 November 2026 Next day: none

On 4–5 November 2026, Democrats secured majorities in both the U.S. House and Senate according to Associated Press and Reuters tallies, flipping seats in several states that voted for Donald Trump in 2024. Vice President JD Vance publicly attributed the losses primarily to the Iran war. In Florida, a gunman killed one employee at a Citibank complex near Tampa before being shot by a security guard. In Spain, the Guardia Civil’s UCO conducted new raids in the Koldo case, and the 30th Ibero-American Summit in Madrid featured a limited appearance by Argentine President Javier Milei and a visit by Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy.

Several U.S. Senate races remained uncalled as of early 5 November. Spanish authorities had not released full details of the new Koldo piece’s evidence. Exact final seat totals for the House depended on outstanding races.

Democrats take control of both chambers of Congress

Associated Press projections and Reuters reporting indicate Democrats will hold at least 54 Senate seats and a substantial House majority after the 3 November elections. The gains include flips in Texas, South Carolina, Kansas, Ohio, Iowa, Maine, and North Carolina. These numbers come from official race calls and exit-poll aggregates; they do not establish a single causal explanation for voter behavior. Claims that the result ends the Trump era or guarantees specific legislative outcomes remain forecasts, not facts. What is checkable is the change in partisan control of the two chambers for the remainder of the presidential term.

Vance states the Iran war was the main reason for Republican losses

Vice President JD Vance wrote on X that “the reason we lost is the war” and that any honest discussion must begin there. President Trump acknowledged the war was a factor while stating he would make the same decision again. These are statements by the two officials; they do not independently measure the war’s share of the vote swing. Exit polls cited by several outlets listed the war, prices, and other issues among voter concerns. The posts are primary evidence of the officials’ framing, not of the underlying causal weights.

One employee killed and gunman shot by security at Tampa-area Citibank complex

Hillsborough County Sheriff’s Office officials stated that a man drove a vehicle into an outdoor dining area at the Citibank Center, shot a woman, and was then shot by an armed security guard in the cafeteria. Both the woman (identified by the company as an employee) and the suspect died. No official motive or prior relationship has been confirmed in the initial briefings. The timeline and locations come from the sheriff’s account; the identities of the deceased had not been publicly released at the time of the reports used here.

La UCO registra el domicilio de Ábalos y detiene a seis personas en una nueva pieza del caso Koldo

Fuentes de la investigación y agencias confirmaron que la Unidad Central Operativa de la Guardia Civil realizó registros, incluida la vivienda del exministro José Luis Ábalos en Valencia, y detuvo a seis personas en Madrid, Córdoba, Valencia y Sevilla. La pieza separada fue abierta por el juez Ismael Moreno tras un informe de la UCO. Los hechos investigados se refieren a presuntas irregularidades en contratos de obra pública. Las detenciones y registros son hechos confirmados; la existencia de delitos y la culpabilidad de las personas detenidas son cuestiones que deben resolverse en el procedimiento judicial.

La XXX Cumbre Iberoamericana en Madrid, con participación limitada de Milei y visita de Zelenski

La cumbre reunió a representantes de 22 países. El presidente argentino Javier Milei no asistió a la inauguración ni a la cena real y limitó su presencia a la sesión plenaria, donde equiparó socialismo y fascismo en términos de política económica. El presidente ucraniano Volodímir Zelenski llegó a Madrid para reuniones bilaterales al margen de la cumbre. El rey Felipe VI pidió a los líderes ceñirse a una “partitura” común. Los discursos y ausencias son hechos registrados; las interpretaciones sobre el clima político de la reunión dependen de la selección de citas.
