Thanksgiving storm leaves hundreds of thousands without power on East Coast

A coastal storm brought high winds and heavy rain to the US East Coast on Thanksgiving Day 26 November, producing peak power outages approaching one million customers according to PowerOutage.us and causing hundreds of flight cancellations. National Weather Service reports cited gusts of 50–70 mph in several Mid-Atlantic and Northeastern states. States of emergency were declared in New York and New Jersey. By early Friday the number of outages had declined but remained in the hundreds of thousands. Wire reports and utility trackers agree on the peak magnitude and the geographic concentration in Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Maryland and Virginia. The precise residual outage count and the time required for full restoration are still updating.
