United States · weather · 27 November 2026

A Thanksgiving coastal storm produced peak power outages near one million customers on the East Coast.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

A powerful storm system affected the US East Coast on Thursday 26 November 2026, the Thanksgiving holiday. According to PowerOutage.us data cited by multiple outlets, the number of utility customers without power peaked near 961,000 at 7:30 p.m. ET and stood at more than 908,000 later that evening. The largest concentrations were reported in Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Maryland, Virginia and Missouri. FlightAware recorded hundreds of cancellations and thousands of delays, with major airports in the New York and Washington areas among those affected. The National Weather Service reported or forecast wind gusts of 50 to 70 mph across parts of Virginia, Maryland, Delaware, New Jersey, New York and coastal New England. Coastal flooding occurred in low-lying communities, compounded by king tides. States of emergency were in effect in New York and New Jersey.

By early Friday 27 November the number of outages had declined but remained in the hundreds of thousands. Conditions were forecast to improve gradually, though restoration work continued. These figures and geographic patterns appear consistently in Reuters, BBC and AP-based reporting.
