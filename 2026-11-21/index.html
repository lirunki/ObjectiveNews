Federal court stays Army plan to detonate lead azide in Iowa

The U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit issued an administrative stay of the EPA’s emergency RCRA permit authorizing the in-place detonation of 68 barrels of degraded lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant. The Army had postponed the blast from 20 November to 23 November citing weather and stability risks. Environmental groups argue the short notice and potential lead dust exposure require fuller review. The stay is temporary until the court rules on the emergency motion; the Army and EPA maintain that controlled detonation is the safest option given the material’s sensitivity. The plant’s location, the barrel count, and the permit dates are checkable against EPA and Army statements. Claims that the blast is “imminent and unavoidable” or that it poses “zero risk” are characterizations, not independent measurements of airborne lead.

Federal jury convicts man of starting the 2025 Palisades fire

A second federal jury found Jonathan Rinderknecht guilty of two arson-related counts for lighting a brush fire on 1 January 2025 that later reignited as the Palisades fire, which killed 12 people and destroyed more than 6,800 structures. The first trial ended in a mistrial. Prosecutors presented evidence of the defendant’s location, a lighter with matching DNA, and the exclusion of alternative causes. The defense argued fireworks or other sources could not be ruled out. The verdict, the charge names, the death toll, and the structure count are agreed across Reuters, AP, BBC and the U.S. Attorney’s statements. Sentencing is set for 17 March 2027; the maximum exposure is up to 40 years. The conviction establishes legal responsibility under the charged statutes; it does not itself determine civil liability or the precise physical sequence of every ember.

S&P 500 and Dow enter correction after AI-related sell-off

On 20 November the S&P 500 fell 3.7 percent and the Dow 1.93 percent, placing both more than 10 percent below recent record highs and confirming a correction. The Nasdaq Composite dropped 5.28 percent and erased its 2026 gains. Anthropic shares declined about 9 percent on their second trading day. Reuters, CBS and NBC match on the closing levels and the correction threshold. Commentary that the move proves an AI bubble or that valuations are now “fair” is interpretation; the index percentages and the consecutive-loss streak are exchange data. Nvidia and other semiconductor names led the decline; the broader market outside technology was less affected.

Sánchez anuncia la prórroga del impuesto a la banca hasta 2027 para vivienda

El presidente del Gobierno anunció el 21 de noviembre que el Consejo de Ministros del 24 de noviembre aprobará la prórroga del impuesto a la banca para 2027, con una recaudación estimada de 1.400 millones de euros destinados íntegramente a políticas de vivienda. El gravamen expiraba a finales de 2026. Fuentes gubernamentales señalan que esa cifra podría financiar más de 6.000 viviendas asequibles, rehabilitar 52.000 y bonificar a unos 120.000 inquilinos. El anuncio, la cifra de 1.400 millones y la vinculación a vivienda son verificables en las declaraciones de Sánchez y en los medios. Que la medida “resuelve el problema de la vivienda” o que constituye “justicia fiscal” son valoraciones políticas, no resultados contables aún publicados en el BOE.

Feijóo reúne a más de 12.000 personas en la Plaza de Toros de Valencia

El 21 de noviembre Alberto Núñez Feijóo clausuró un mitin del PP en la Plaza de Toros de Valencia al que el partido atribuye más de 12.000 asistentes, el más multitudinario de su campaña para las elecciones del 29 de noviembre. El candidato pidió no confiarse tras el cara a cara del 19 de noviembre y usó metáforas taurinas. Europa Press, ABC y El Mundo coinciden en la cifra de aforo reportada por el PP y en los acompañantes: el presidente de la Generalitat, la alcaldesa de Valencia y el presidente provincial. La asistencia exacta no ha sido auditada de forma independiente; la declaración de “llenazo” es una afirmación del partido. El efecto sobre el voto del 29-N sigue siendo desconocido.
