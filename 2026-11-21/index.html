On 20–21 November a federal jury in Los Angeles convicted Jonathan Rinderknecht of two arson counts linked to the January 2025 Palisades Fire that killed 12 people and destroyed more than 6,800 structures. Jury convicts man of starting the 2025 Palisades Fire

A second federal jury found Jonathan Rinderknecht, 30, guilty on both counts of destruction of property by means of fire and arson affecting interstate commerce. Prosecutors said he lit the Lachman fire on 1 January 2025; it smoldered underground and reignited on 7 January under Santa Ana winds as the Palisades Fire. The first trial ended in a 10-2 deadlock for acquittal. Reuters, BBC, AP and CNN match on the verdict, the prior mistrial, the death toll of 12, and the structure count above 6,800. Sentencing is set for 17 March 2027. Defense claims of fireworks as an alternative cause were rejected by the jury; they remain a theory, not a finding.

Appeals court stays Army plan to detonate lead azide in Iowa

The Eighth Circuit issued a one-sentence administrative stay on the EPA emergency permit that authorized open detonation of about 68 barrels (roughly 9,500–9,700 pounds) of degraded lead azide at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant. The Army had already postponed the blast from 20 November to 23 November, citing a forecast cold snap that could further destabilize the material. Environmental groups argued inadequate notice and alternatives. Des Moines Register, CNN and Army statements agree on the stay, the quantity, the location in Middletown, and the prior postponement. Whether the court will lift the stay before colder weather arrives is still open.

Sánchez anuncia prórroga del impuesto a la banca hasta 2027 para vivienda

Pedro Sánchez dijo en Onda Cero que el Consejo de Ministros del 24 de noviembre aprobará la prórroga del impuesto a la banca durante 2027, con una recaudación estimada de 1.400 millones de euros destinados íntegramente a vivienda: más de 6.000 viviendas asequibles, rehabilitación de unas 52.000 y bonificaciones a casi 120.000 inquilinos. El anuncio llega a ocho días de las elecciones del 29 de noviembre. El País, RTVE y 20minutos coinciden en la cifra, el destino y la fecha del Consejo. Que el impuesto sea “justicia fiscal” o que se mantenga toda la legislatura son afirmaciones del candidato, no hechos ya aprobados.
