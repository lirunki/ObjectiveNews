Los Angeles · arson · 21 November 2026

Federal jury convicts Jonathan Rinderknecht of starting the 2025 Palisades Fire.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

On 20 November 2026 a federal jury in the Central District of California returned guilty verdicts on both counts against Jonathan Rinderknecht, 30. The charges were destruction of property by means of fire and arson affecting property used in interstate commerce. Prosecutors said Rinderknecht used a lighter to ignite the Lachman fire in the early hours of 1 January 2025 near the Pacific Palisades. That fire burned underground in root systems for six days and then reignited on 7 January under strong Santa Ana winds, becoming the Palisades Fire. The fire killed 12 people and destroyed more than 6,800 structures across Pacific Palisades, Topanga and Malibu. Reuters, BBC, AP via CNN, the Los Angeles Times and NBC Los Angeles all report the same verdict date, the same prior mistrial, the same death and structure counts, and the same sentencing date of 17 March 2027.

The first trial in June 2026 ended in a mistrial when jurors deadlocked 10-2 in favor of acquittal. Judge Anne Hwang ordered a retrial. In the second trial prosecutors narrowed the presentation, focusing on evidence that Rinderknecht was the only person in the area at the relevant time and that other ignition sources, including fireworks, had been eliminated by investigators. The jury deliberated roughly two days before convicting. Rinderknecht had pleaded not guilty. He has been in federal custody since his arrest in October 2025. The statutory maximum is 40 years; the mandatory minimum is five years on each count.

What is only a quote

First Assistant U.S. Attorney Bill Essayli said the verdict “delivers a measure of justice for the families who lost loved ones.” That is a prosecutor’s characterization. Defense attorney Steven Haney said outside court that the team was “disappointed” and would examine possible grounds for appeal. A juror told reporters that inconsistencies in Rinderknecht’s account of his whereabouts were decisive. Those statements are quotes. They are not independent findings of fact beyond the verdict itself.

Mechanism a reader needs

Wildfires can smolder in duff and root systems for days after surface flames are extinguished. When wind and low humidity return, the heat can re-emerge as a full-scale blaze. Investigators used camera timelines, cell-phone location data, and elimination of other ignition sources to place Rinderknecht at the origin of the Lachman fire. The defense offered fireworks as an alternative explanation. The jury rejected that theory. Holdover fires are a documented phenomenon in California chaparral; they do not require a second human ignition.

The Palisades Fire burned 23,448 acres according to Cal Fire figures cited in trial coverage. Damage assessments vary slightly by outlet between 6,800 and 6,845 structures. Those differences are rounding or timing of counts, not contradictions. The human toll of 12 deaths is consistent across major wires.

What this story is not

It is not a finding that every ignition theory has been closed forever. An appeal remains possible. It is not a statement about the broader causes of the January 2025 Los Angeles fires, which also included the Eaton Fire.
