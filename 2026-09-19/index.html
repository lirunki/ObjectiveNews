Trump announces ban of CNN, MS NOW and Politico from White House

On 18 September President Trump posted that he was banning CNN, MS NOW and Politico from the White House “effective immediately” because of what he described as constant “fake news.” He later told reporters the decision was cumulative rather than tied to any single story and threatened that other outlets could follow. Journalists from the three organisations remained on the White House grounds that afternoon; neither the White House nor the outlets reported physical removal of credentials by close of business. The outlets stated they would defend First Amendment rights. Enforcement mechanics—credential revocation, pool exclusion, or ground-access denial—were not detailed by the administration. Courts have previously limited viewpoint-based exclusion of the press from public forums; the legal durability of this ban is therefore an open question.

U.S., Denmark and Greenland reach security deal expanding American military presence

President Trump announced on 18 September that the United States had reached an agreement with Denmark and Greenland giving the U.S. permanent ability to expand its military presence and to block adversary bases or sensitive investments on the island. Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen and Greenlandic leaders described the same instrument as recognising the Kingdom’s sovereignty and Greenland’s right to self-determination. The text is scheduled for signature at the UN General Assembly the following week and still requires parliamentary steps. Existing U.S. rights under the 1951 defence agreement already permit basing at Pituffik; the new deal appears to codify expanded access and investment screening. Claims of “permanent control over security and all other needs” remain political framing until the signed text is public.

All 50 states join or apply for GENEROUS Medicaid most-favored-nation drug model

CMS announced that all 50 states, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico have applied to participate in the GENEROUS model, which seeks most-favored-nation net prices for selected Medicaid outpatient drugs via supplemental rebates. Forty states and Puerto Rico had already signed agreements; the remainder have until 30 September to finalise. The White House Council of Economic Advisers estimated roughly $64 billion in combined federal and state savings over ten years. Manufacturers that previously agreed to MFN terms with the administration are expected to supply the model. Exact drug lists, net prices after rebates, and the distribution of savings between federal and state budgets remain partially opaque pending further CMS documentation.

CBP begins wall-panel installation in Big Bend 1 sector of west Texas

U.S. Customs and Border Protection confirmed that installation of 30-foot steel bollard panels is underway in the 47-mile Big Bend 1 project area in Hudspeth County, west Texas. The work is part of a larger set of contracts covering roughly 175 miles across the broader Big Bend region and is distinct from the paused vehicle-barrier and technology project inside Big Bend National Park. Local landowners, ranchers and environmental groups have opposed the remote-sector wall on cost, environmental and traffic-volume grounds. CBP states it is now averaging about 12 miles of barrier per week system-wide. Exact panel counts already set and the schedule for the remaining Big Bend segments were not released in the 18 September statements.

Fiscalía abre expedientes de protección tras denuncias de menores en centros de Ceuta

Varias menores alojadas en centros de acogida de Ceuta han denunciado abusos y vejaciones. La Fiscalía ha abierto expedientes de protección. El contexto es la crisis migratoria iniciada a finales de julio, con miles de personas aún en la ciudad autónoma y traslados parciales a instalaciones portuarias y del barrio de Los Rosales. Las cifras de permanencia siguen divergiendo entre la administración local y el Gobierno central. El artículo completo está en español y separa hechos verificables de alegaciones aún bajo investigación.
