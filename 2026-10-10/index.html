Isaias made landfall near Destin as a Category 2 and weakened to a post-tropical cyclone

The National Hurricane Center’s landfall statement places the center near Destin, Florida, at approximately 8:30 p.m. CDT on 9 October with 105 mph sustained winds. Reuters and CBS report the system was Category 2 at landfall and had become post-tropical with 65 mph winds by early Saturday while crossing southern Alabama. PowerOutage.us figures cited by multiple desks show hundreds of thousands of customers without power in Florida and Alabama. Storm-surge and flooding warnings continued inland. The still is a 1973 NARA photograph of Destin beach, not the event.

ICE agent shot Oscar Belgal in New York while a 5-year-old was in the car

DHS confirmed an ICE officer discharged a weapon during a targeted enforcement operation in the Bronx/Marble Hill area on Thursday afternoon. City officials identified the wounded man as Oscar Belgal, 28, from the Dominican Republic, and said a 5-year-old child in the vehicle was unharmed. NYPD Commissioner Jessica Tisch described plainclothes officers approaching as Belgal pulled into a parking space and then firing after the car moved forward. ICE officials said agents believed their lives were in danger because the vehicle was used as a weapon. No public video of the shots themselves has been released in these accounts. Navi Pillay received the 2026 Nobel Peace Prize

Reuters and BBC report the citation and her reaction in Nuremberg. The committee chair noted international law institutions are under pressure.
