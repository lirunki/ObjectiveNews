Police: 11 shot near Florida A&M University stadium after altercation

Tallahassee police said the shooting occurred just before 8 p.m. Saturday near Perry and Manatee streets, adjacent to Bragg Memorial Stadium during homecoming weekend. Eleven people sustained gunshot wounds; a twelfth person, described as a grandmother of a victim, was injured while fleeing. Six adults and six juveniles were among the injured; the youngest was 11. At least three had serious injuries, one critical. Police said a physical altercation among two or three individuals preceded the gunfire and described it as isolated. No arrests were announced by early Sunday. The university stated the incident was near campus and the campus remained safe. Numbers and timing come from police statements reported by Reuters, AP, NBC and local outlets; motive and suspect identity are not yet public.

These are statements by the president; they are not confirmation that an order has been given or that strikes occurred. Mossos detienen a 128 personas, 72 menores, tras disturbios en Barcelona

Una concentración no autorizada convocada por redes sociales, especialmente TikTok, reunió a centenares de jóvenes en la plaza de Catalunya el sábado por la tarde. Según el balance de los Mossos d’Esquadra del domingo, se detuvo a 128 personas (122 hombres, 6 mujeres), de las cuales 72 eran menores. Se vandalizaron o saquearon 23 comercios, los bomberos actuaron en 33 incendios de mobiliario urbano y 14–15 agentes resultaron heridos, cuatro de gravedad. Solo dos detenidos tenían antecedentes por desórdenes públicos. La policía describió la violencia como “gratuita” y a los participantes como desorganizados. Las cifras son las publicadas por el cuerpo; la identidad de organizadores y los cargos concretos siguen en investigación. Feijóo y otros líderes vincularon los hechos a la campaña electoral; esas vinculaciones son declaraciones políticas.
