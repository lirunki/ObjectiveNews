Florida · public safety · 25 October 2026

Tallahassee police said 11 people were shot near Florida A&M University’s Bragg Memorial Stadium after an altercation on Saturday night.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

Tallahassee Police Chief Lawrence Revell stated that a shooting occurred just before 8 p.m. on 24 October 2026 near the intersection of Perry and Manatee streets, close to Bragg Memorial Stadium on the Florida A&M University campus during homecoming weekend. Eleven people sustained gunshot wounds. A twelfth person, identified as the grandmother of one of the victims, was injured while fleeing the scene. Of the 12 injured, six were adults and six were juveniles. The youngest victim was 11 years old. At least three victims sustained serious injuries, with one in critical condition; the remainder were described as stable. Police said preliminary information indicated that a physical altercation among two or three individuals preceded the gunfire and characterized the incident as isolated. No arrests had been announced by early Sunday. Florida A&M University issued a statement that the incident occurred near campus, that police were investigating, and that the campus remained safe.

Reuters, the Associated Press, NBC News, ABC News, and local Tallahassee outlets reported these figures, the time, the location relative to the stadium, the breakdown of adults and juveniles, the youngest age, the injury status categories, and the absence of immediate arrests. The university’s statement was quoted consistently.

What is only a quote

Statements by the police chief that the event was “devastating” or that he stood before the public “devastated” are expressions of reaction. Descriptions of the altercation as involving “two or three individuals” are preliminary police characterizations; they have not been tested in court. Claims linking the shooting directly to the football game outcome or to homecoming activities beyond the timing and location are not supported by the police statements reported. Any assertion about motive, gang affiliation, or a specific suspect remains unverified as of the initial reports.

Mechanism a reader needs

Mass-casualty shootings in public spaces trigger immediate police response, medical transport, scene control, and subsequent investigation by specialized units such as a violent crimes unit. Victim counts can be revised as people present for treatment or as injuries are reclassified. Juvenile victims are handled under different privacy and charging rules. Homecoming events concentrate people in a limited geographic area; an altercation that escalates to gunfire in such a setting produces multiple victims even if the number of shooters is small. Certification of the exact number of gunshot wounds versus other injuries requires medical confirmation; initial police figures are operational counts.

Florida A&M University is a public historically Black land-grant institution in Tallahassee. Bragg Memorial Stadium is its football venue. The university’s statement separates the incident from the campus proper while acknowledging proximity. Local emergency services and the police department are the primary sources for casualty and scene information; national wires rely on those briefings and official statements.

What this story is not

It is not a completed criminal investigation. It is not a finding that any particular person fired the shots. It is not evidence of a broader campus threat beyond the police description of an isolated altercation.
