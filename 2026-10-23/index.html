Day summary: deposition order, Virginia indictment, Spanish campaign exchanges

On 22 October a U.S. magistrate judge ordered President Donald Trump to sit for a deposition by 11 December in his $10 billion defamation suit against the BBC, warning of possible sanctions including dismissal if he does not. Judge orders Trump deposition in BBC defamation case by 11 December

A federal magistrate in Miami ordered President Trump to appear for deposition by 11 December in the $10 billion suit he filed against the BBC over editing of his 6 January 2021 speech in a Panorama documentary. BBC lawyers told the court Trump had canceled a previously set 16 October date, citing national importance while he was campaigning; the judge set the deadline and warned that non-compliance could lead to sanctions up to dismissal unless the discovery cutoff of 18 December is extended. The order is a procedural ruling about when the plaintiff must testify under oath; it does not decide whether the BBC’s edit was defamatory or what damages, if any, are owed. The underlying factual dispute—whether the spliced clips created a false impression that Trump directed violence—remains for later stages.
