Macy’s moves 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday

Macy’s, the City of New York, and NBC agreed on Wednesday evening to move the 100th annual parade from Thursday morning to 8:30 a.m. Saturday. The National Weather Service had issued a high-wind warning for Thursday with gusts potentially exceeding the 34 mph threshold at which large balloons must be grounded under rules dating to 1998. Balloons that had begun inflation on Wednesday were deflated and returned to a New Jersey facility for re-inflation Friday. The postponement is the first for weather; the parade was suspended only during World War II. Saturday’s forecast is clearer though still windy. No independent measurement of Thursday’s peak winds at parade height is yet public because the event did not occur. Organizers stated that safety of participants and spectators was the priority; claims that every performing group can rearrange travel remain operational statements, not completed logistics.

USTR proposes Section 301 tariffs on 14 trading partners

On 25 November USTR announced affirmative determinations in 14 of 16 Section 301 investigations into structural excess capacity practices and proposed additional tariffs ranging from 15 percent (EU, Japan, Korea, Switzerland, Taiwan) to up to 35 percent (Vietnam), with China at up to 20 percent on most goods net of existing forced-labor duties. The proposals are subject to public comment through 5 January 2027 and hearings beginning 25 January; no effective date has been set. Rates are calibrated against existing bilateral agreements and a Busan truce with China that expires 10 January 2027; alternative higher rates are proposed if consultations fail. Official notices and trade-law analyses agree on the list of economies and the proposed ranges. Whether the final rates will match the proposals, and how they will net against MFN and prior Section 301 duties, remains for the comment process and any subsequent Federal Register notice.

Army reports successful Iowa lead-azide detonation; EPA sees zero airborne lead

The Army executed an in-place controlled detonation of approximately 9,700 pounds of crystallized lead azide in 68 drums at the Middletown plant on the morning of 25 November after the Eighth Circuit restored the EPA emergency permit. Acting Army Secretary Adam Telle and Maj. Gen. Eric Shirley described the operation as exceeding expectations, with all barrels destroyed, no secondary explosions, and no infrastructure damage. EPA real-time air monitors reported zero detection of airborne lead in the hours afterward; an all-clear was issued for residents. Surface and soil sampling continues. Official statements and wire reports agree on the quantity, location, and the immediate air-monitoring result. Longer-term deposition measurements and any residual risk assessments are not yet public.

Buque cisterna Med Emre se hunde cerca de Ibiza; un tripulante fallece

El buque tanque químico Med Emre, con bandera de Malta y que transportaba hasta 7.000 toneladas métricas de gasóleo de Algeciras a Barcelona, comunicó una vía de agua y escora de unos 90 grados la noche del 25 de noviembre y se volcó o hundió parcialmente a unas 25-30 millas náuticas al norte de Ibiza. Los 17 tripulantes fueron rescatados; uno falleció después en el hospital Can Misses de Ibiza y otros seis sufrieron heridas leves, principalmente hipotermia. España activó el nivel 2 del plan nacional de contingencia por contaminación marítima. Fuentes oficiales y cables coinciden en la bandera, la carga aproximada, la ubicación y el balance de víctimas. No hay confirmación independiente de si se ha producido un vertido de gasóleo; la evaluación de Salvamento Marítimo continúa.
