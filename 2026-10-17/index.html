Teacher tackled and armed father shot the Bend elementary school attacker

Bend Police Chief Mike Krantz said on 16 October that a teacher in the special-needs classroom threw a phone and then wrestled with the 16-year-old shooter, slowing him, after which Kaylub Giamanco, a father of two students, entered and shot the attacker with a legal pistol just before police arrived. Three children aged 5, 8 and 11 and teacher Megan Lambert, 25, were killed; a 7-year-old and another teacher were wounded. The shooter, identified as Griffin John II, a former special-education student, had entered by shooting through locked doors after firing at a truck outside. These sequence details are consistent across AP, Reuters and BBC reporting of the chief’s briefing. Katie Zacharia leaves White House press secretary post after five days

The White House confirmed on 16 October that Katie Zacharia was transitioning out of the press secretary role after a five-day tenure that began with her appointment announcement and included one short briefing on 13 October that took questions from three outlets. Trump told reporters he had advised her she could reconsider. Questions had arisen about whether she passed a bar exam after describing herself as a lawyer. Trump waives key Graham Act Russia sanctions two days before deadline

On 16 October the White House published an executive order implementing the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 while waiving application of certain sanctions, restrictions and duties, with a certification that the waiver is in the national interest. The law, signed 18 September, set an 18 October implementation deadline for measures including tariffs on major Russian energy buyers. Zelenskyy and some lawmakers criticized the move; a Russian envoy called it historic. The order text is the checkable primary document. Norte Fire burns coastal homes in Orange County under Santa Ana winds

Orange County Fire Authority said the Norte Fire, which started in San Clemente and was driven into San Juan Capistrano by winds, had burned several hundred acres and damaged or destroyed at least six homes by Friday evening, with thousands under evacuation orders. Two firefighters were treated for heat-related injuries. Cause was not stated. These figures come from AP and local authority briefings. Pope Leo XIV cleared for discharge four days after lung-cancer surgery

The Vatican press office and the pope’s doctors said on 17 October that blood tests, a chest X-ray and clinical assessment showed Pope Leo XIV fit for discharge from Gemelli hospital. He had undergone removal of the lower lobe of his right lung on 13 October after a nodule was confirmed as adenocarcinoma. He is scheduled to lead the Angelus from the Vatican on 18 October. These statements match Reuters, AP and Vatican communiqués. The image is a type photograph of St. Peter’s Square, not a picture of the hospital or the pontiff.
