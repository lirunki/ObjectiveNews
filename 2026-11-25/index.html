Army detonates degraded lead azide at Iowa plant

The checkable act is the Army’s execution of an in-place controlled detonation of approximately 9,700 pounds of crystallized lead azide in 68 drums at the Middletown plant. Official releases and wire reports agree on the quantity, location, and the technical conclusion that transport or chemical neutralization carried higher risk of uncontrolled explosion. Court clearance came the previous day when the Eighth Circuit lifted its stay. Local and state officials had prepared monitoring; post-blast sampling results are not yet public. Claims about the exact size of the lead plume remain model projections, not measured deposition.

Ábrego García arrives in Spain after voluntary departure from the US

DHS stated that Kilmar Ábrego García self-deported to Spain. AP and other wires confirmed his arrival at Madrid’s airport on 25 November after a commercial flight. His lawyers described the move as voluntary and temporary; DHS said he received no incentives and is barred from re-entry. The prior March 2025 removal to El Salvador was labeled an administrative error by officials at the time. The legal status that allowed the 2025 deportation despite an earlier court order remains the background fact; the precise negotiated terms of the current departure are not fully public.

Récord de llegadas de migrantes a El Hierro en 24 horas

Los servicios de salvamento marítimo registraron más de 2.100 llegadas en un periodo de 24 horas, la mayoría en El Hierro, superando el récord anterior de unos 1.500. Una persona murió en una de las embarcaciones y otra dio a luz durante la travesía. El Gobierno central señala coordinación con Senegal y Gambia y refuerzo de controles; el Gobierno canario y el PP canario describen la situación como insostenible y piden intervención de Frontex. Las cifras proceden de datos de rescate; las valoraciones políticas son citas de cada parte.

Six die in Cessna 210 crash near Horn Island, Mississippi

The U.S. Coast Guard reported that divers located the remains of all six people aboard a Cessna 210 that crashed into the water about two miles northeast of Horn Island on 24 November. Flight tracking showed a rapid descent after departure from New Orleans Lakefront bound for Fort Myers. The Jackson County coroner later identified the victims as a family group from Florida. NTSB will lead the investigation; no probable cause has been released. The aircraft type and location are consistent across Coast Guard and local reporting.
