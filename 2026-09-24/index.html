Judge Kelly orders temporary restoration of CNN, MS NOW and POLITICO White House hard passes

The checkable act is a TRO from Judge Timothy Kelly of the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia, issued early 24 September. It directs the government to immediately return, reinstate and restore hard passes revoked on or about 18 September and lasts 14 days unless modified. Kelly, a 2017 Trump appointee, said the outlets were likely to succeed on a Fifth Amendment due-process claim because the government does not dispute that the journalists received no pre-revocation notice or hearing. He treated the national-security rationale as weakly supported on the present record. That is a court finding on likelihood of success, not a final judgment on the First Amendment retaliation claim. Later reporting that reporters were still blocked is a separate operational fact.

Navy letter: eight suicide attempts across Abraham Lincoln strike group; none fatal

Acting Navy Secretary Hung Cao wrote Senator Gillibrand that eight suicide attempts occurred across the Lincoln carrier strike group since deployment began. The letter states no service member died by suicide in that set. It specifies one August overboard recovery and one March attempt stopped by shipmates. Families and some members of Congress describe morale and logistics problems; Cao acknowledges localized piping and desalination issues while denying a generalized crisis framing. The eight-count is an official number in a congressional letter.

First recorded tropical sea-turtle nest reported on a Southern California beach

Multiple desks reported a tropical sea turtle nesting on a Southern California beach as a first in the modern West Coast record, with scientists quoted linking the event to warmer water. NOAA Fisheries is the appropriate primary agency for species and nest confirmation. A single nest is a biological observation. It is not, by itself, a completed climate attribution study. Egg fate and species identification remain items to confirm from the agency.

Desahucio ejecutado de Maricarmen, 87 años, en el distrito de Retiro (Madrid)

El hecho comprobable es el lanzamiento judicial del 23 de septiembre de 2026 en la calle Alcalde Sáinz de Baranda. María del Carmen Abascal, de 87 años, salió en camilla hacia el Hospital Gregorio Marañón. La propiedad, Urbagestión, rechazó mediaciones y ofertas de pago de terceros, según la defensa. El contrato se remonta a 1956. Que el caso sea símbolo de la crisis de vivienda es una interpretación política; no sustituye el auto judicial. Ayuso atribuye el acto al cumplimiento de una sentencia; Sánchez atribuye dejación a Madrid. Ambas son citas.
