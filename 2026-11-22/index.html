ICE officers arrest two passengers boarding Alaska flight at Seattle-Tacoma Airport

ICE stated that officers conducted a targeted operation on 21 November to arrest Ricardo Larrondo Mendoza, described as a Mexican national who overstayed a visa. During the arrest on the jet bridge of Alaska flight 1306 to Los Angeles, a second person, Sofia Hernandez Mejia, also described by ICE as a visa overstay, was arrested after ICE said she threw hot coffee at officers. Washington Governor Bob Ferguson called the scene “distressing” and said ICE was “out of control.” The Port of Seattle said the operation occurred without its knowledge and was reviewing protocols. Video circulated on social media showing a confrontation; ICE posted its account on X. Independent verification of the individuals’ exact immigration status and of the coffee-throwing claim rests on the agency’s statement and contemporaneous video. The flight was delayed. No court filings were publicly available at the time of writing.

COP31 climate talks in Antalya end without agreement after fossil-fuel language dispute

The UN climate conference that opened on 9 November in Antalya ran into overtime and closed on 22 November without adoption of a cover decision. COP31 President Murat Kurum said a deal had not been approved. Ireland’s minister Darragh O’Brien, speaking for the EU, rejected the final draft because it did not mention fossil fuels, the primary driver of warming according to the IPCC. Australia’s Chris Bowen, president of negotiations, called the outcome disappointing. Saudi Arabia and other producers had opposed explicit phase-out language, according to multiple reports. The last full failure to adopt a decision was Copenhagen 2009. The draft had acknowledged that warming is on course to exceed 1.5 °C. No new text was expected after the rejection.

Campaña electoral española del 29N: mítines de Sánchez y Feijóo a una semana de las urnas

Las elecciones generales españolas se celebran el 29 de noviembre de 2026 para renovar el Congreso (350 escaños) y el Senado. La campaña oficial va del 13 al 27 de noviembre. El 19 de noviembre se celebró el cara a cara entre Pedro Sánchez (PSOE) y Alberto Núñez Feijóo (PP). El domingo 22 ambos coincidieron en actos en Murcia. Sánchez advirtió sobre la “ultraderecha” y Feijóo llamó a pelear el voto. Las encuestas publicadas antes de la prohibición del 24 de noviembre situaban al PP en cabeza en la mayoría de sondeos, con excepción del CIS. No hay resultados oficiales; el voto por correo se puede depositar hasta el 25. Lo que es cita de los candidatos se etiqueta como tal; las cifras de escaños son proyecciones de encuestas, no hechos.

Avería del tren automático entre la T4 y la T4S de Barajas provoca colas; Aena anuncia normalidad

El domingo 22 de noviembre una incidencia técnica en el tren automático operado por Alstom que une la terminal T4 con su satélite T4S del aeropuerto Adolfo Suárez Madrid-Barajas obligó a prestar el servicio con autobuses y generó largas colas de pasajeros. Aena informó en X de la incidencia y recomendó acudir con antelación a los vuelos internacionales. Más tarde el gestor aeroportuario comunicó que el sistema “ya opera con normalidad”. No se publicaron cifras exactas de pasajeros afectados ni de retrasos de vuelos. La causa técnica concreta no se detalló más allá de “incidencia técnica”.
