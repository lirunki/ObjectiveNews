United States · diplomacy · 13–14 October 2026

The White House asked Ambassador Kimberly Guilfoyle to resign after published reports of improper conduct.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

On Tuesday, 13 October 2026, the White House said it had asked US Ambassador to Greece Kimberly Guilfoyle to resign. New press secretary Katie Zacharia stated at her first briefing that the administration had made the request and wished Guilfoyle well. Guilfoyle posted that the allegations against her were false and that she would vigorously defend her name, reputation, and integrity. President Trump told reporters he “didn’t like what we were reading.” No criminal charges have been filed, and no internal investigation findings have been released by the State Department.

The Wall Street Journal had published a series of reports beginning in late September describing messages in which Guilfoyle allegedly asked a Trump donor for help paying a $100,000 credit-card bill in exchange for access, among other claims of improper gifts and influence. Guilfoyle’s lawyers have disputed the reports. Reuters, AP, PBS, and CBS matched the White House request and Guilfoyle’s denial.

What is only a quote

“I didn’t like what we were reading” is a presidential reaction to published stories, not a finding of fact. Guilfoyle’s statement that the allegations are false is a denial, not an independent verification. Claims that she “sold access” remain allegations in the reporting until and unless a court or inspector-general report establishes them.

Mechanism a reader needs

US ambassadors serve at the pleasure of the president. The White House can request a resignation without a public hearing or a published investigation. Confirmation of a successor requires Senate advice and consent. Until a new ambassador is confirmed, the deputy chief of mission typically becomes chargé d’affaires. The Athens post is one of many; the operational continuity of the embassy does not depend on any single political appointee.

Published reports of financial requests before confirmation hearings raise questions about the vetting process and about the line between campaign relationships and official duties. Those questions are separate from the arithmetic of whether a particular wire transfer occurred. The Journal’s reporting is the primary public record of the alleged messages; the White House has not released its own documentary package.

Greece is a NATO ally with energy and defense ties to the United States. An ambassador’s departure does not alter those treaties. It does remove one political channel while the embassy staff continues routine work.

What this story is not

It is not a conviction. It is not a statement that every allegation has been proven or disproven. It is not a claim about Guilfoyle’s personal character beyond the published denial and the White House request. The photograph is a type image of the embassy entrance from 2018, not an event photo from this week.
