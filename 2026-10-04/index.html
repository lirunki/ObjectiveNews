La Policía empezó a levantar parte del asentamiento de la playa del Trampolín

Sunday’s operation is partial because Ceuta does not have beds for the whole settlement. RTVE cites the regional police command for an estimate of more than 3,500 people and describes more than 30 vans, two BMR armored vehicles, and one VAMTAC. Cadena SER’s operational sources say about 700 people were moved and at least 400 were bused to Tarajal for a voluntary return. Infobae adds a health finding of E. coli and enterococci and a stated aim to clear the beach before 15 October. None of the three publishes a written roll-up of who remained. The beach photograph is from May 2025, not the sweep.
