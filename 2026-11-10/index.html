Massie files resolution to impeach Hegseth over Iran war; Pentagon briefing set

Outgoing Republican Rep. Thomas Massie introduced a privileged resolution containing articles of impeachment against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth, citing the conduct of the Iran conflict. Hegseth has scheduled his first Pentagon press briefing in six months for 10 November and has called the effort a political spectacle. The White House has said President Trump stands with Hegseth. The articles are public documents; any House vote occurs under the current Republican majority, and removal would require a two-thirds Senate vote that has not been scheduled. Wire reports match on the filer, the focus on Iran, and the briefing plan; the political prospects remain unvoted.

California voters reject Proposition 40 billionaire wealth tax

The Associated Press projected that California’s Proposition 40, a one-time 5 percent tax on the wealth of the state’s billionaires with proceeds largely directed to health care, failed with approximately 53 percent voting no. The measure was backed by a health-care workers’ union. Official vote tallies continue to be counted, but the AP call is the standard early projection used by wires. No independent audit of the revenue estimate has been published beyond the official fiscal analysis; the political interpretation of the result as a signal on progressive taxation is outside the vote count itself.

U.S.-mediated Russia-Ukraine talks open in Abu Dhabi

Delegations from the United States, Russia, and Ukraine began talks in Abu Dhabi on 10 November aimed at ending the war. The sessions are the first formal round in roughly nine months. AP and other wires confirm the location, the participants, and the stated goal of seeking an end to hostilities. No joint communiqué has been issued; specific proposals on territory, security guarantees, or cease-fire lines remain unconfirmed beyond participant statements. The photograph is a type image of an Abu Dhabi palace complex, not the meeting venue.

El Gobierno aprueba medidas urgentes de seguridad ferroviaria tras Urda; Sánchez no asiste al funeral de Estado

Tras el accidente de alta velocidad en Urda (Toledo) de finales de octubre, el Consejo de Ministros ha aprobado un real decreto-ley con medidas urgentes de seguridad ferroviaria, entre ellas el cómputo de tiempos de conducción y el refuerzo de la supervisión de la Agencia Estatal de Seguridad Ferroviaria. El funeral de Estado en Toledo se celebra con la asistencia de los Reyes y varios ministros, pero sin el presidente Pedro Sánchez, quien ha alegado su laicismo. Los cables coinciden en las medidas aprobadas y en la ausencia de Sánchez; el número exacto de heridos que permanecen ingresados se actualiza diariamente y no está cerrado en todos los reportes.

El Gobierno declara la emergencia migratoria en Canarias y refuerza el rescate en El Hierro

El Gobierno de España ha declarado la emergencia migratoria en Canarias y anunciado el envío de un barco de rescate adicional a El Hierro, donde se concentra la mayoría de las llegadas. Las cifras de los primeros días del mes superan las 4.500 personas. Los reportes coinciden en la declaración de emergencia y en el refuerzo de medios; el origen exacto de cada embarcación y el número de personas fallecidas en la ruta atlántica siguen basándose en declaraciones de autoridades y ONG, no en un censo cerrado.
