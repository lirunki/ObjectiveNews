United States · Spain · 3 November 2026

Election day in the United States; Urda train victims fully identified in Spain; limited strikes reported on Qeshm.

On 3 November 2026, Americans voted in midterm elections that will determine control of the House and Senate for the remainder of President Trump’s second term. In Spain, authorities confirmed that all 69 people killed in the 28 October high-speed train collision near Urda had been identified. U.S. officials said limited defensive strikes had been conducted against targets on Iran’s Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz.

What remains unknown includes final vote tallies in several competitive Senate races, the precise cause of the Learjet 60 crash in Palm Beach County that killed nine people on 1 November, and whether the Qeshm strikes will lead to further Iranian responses. Investigations into the Urda collision continue under the independent accident authority.

Voters cast ballots in 2026 midterm elections that will set congressional control.

Polls opened across the United States on 3 November for midterm elections. Control of both chambers of Congress is at stake after two years of the second Trump administration. Early voting totals were high in several states. The Supreme Court had rejected a Trump administration request concerning California mail ballots the day before. Outcomes in Senate races in Ohio, Florida, and other states will determine the majority. Claims by candidates about the meaning of early returns are not yet final results.

NTSB says Learjet that crashed in Florida may have begun breaking apart before impact; nine killed.

A Learjet 60 flying from Austin, Texas, to the Bahamas crashed on 1 November near Greenacres in Palm Beach County, killing all nine people aboard. NTSB investigators said on 2 November that there was evidence of some in-flight breakup. The aircraft descended more than 27,000 feet in roughly one minute. Families identified most of the passengers, including a Texas technology entrepreneur, his wife, and their two young children. The cause remains under investigation; pilot reports of ice and turbulence are preliminary.

U.S. officials say limited defensive strikes hit targets on Iran’s Qeshm Island.

U.S. officials told reporters that American forces conducted limited strikes on Qeshm Island on 2 November, describing them as defensive responses to Iranian surface-to-air missile fire against U.S. aircraft. Iranian state media reported explosions on the island. The action occurred the day before U.S. midterm elections. President Trump had previously described recent activity as not constituting a return to major combat. Whether additional strikes or Iranian retaliation will follow is not yet known from public statements.

Autoridades identifican a las 69 víctimas del accidente de Urda; Puente anuncia reducción de frecuencias.

El Centro Integrado de Datos confirmó la identificación de las 69 personas fallecidas en la colisión de alta velocidad ocurrida el 28 de octubre cerca de Urda (Toledo). El ministro de Transportes, Óscar Puente, anunció un plan de refuerzo de seguridad y una reducción de frecuencias en la línea Madrid-Sevilla hasta la instalación de un nuevo sistema. La oposición exige su dimisión. La investigación técnica sigue abierta; las hipótesis incluyen un fallo del sistema LZB y autorizaciones del centro de control de Adif.
