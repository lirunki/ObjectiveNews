United States · elections · 3 November 2026

Americans voted on 3 November in midterm elections that will determine control of the House and Senate.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

Voting took place on Tuesday, 3 November 2026, across all 50 states and the District of Columbia for all 435 House seats and approximately one-third of Senate seats. Early voting and mail ballots had been cast in large numbers in the preceding weeks. Multiple outlets reported that the election is widely viewed as a referendum on the second Trump administration’s first two years. The Supreme Court on 2 November rejected a request by the administration concerning the handling of certain California ballots. No final national results were available at the time of this report because polls in the western states remained open.

Competitive Senate races include those in Ohio, Florida, Michigan, and several others. House control is expected by analysts to be close. Turnout figures will be compiled by state election offices after polls close. Claims made by candidates or parties about “momentum” or “fraud” before all ballots are counted are statements, not verified outcomes.

What is only a quote

Statements by President Trump, Vice President Vance, or Democratic leaders about the importance of the election or predicted outcomes are campaign speech. They are not election results. Early exit-poll estimates released by networks are projections based on samples and can shift.

Mechanism a reader needs

Midterm elections occur two years after a presidential election. The party that holds the White House historically loses House seats on average, though the size of the loss varies with presidential approval and economic conditions. Senate races are staggered; only some seats are on the ballot. Certified results come from state canvassing boards, not from cable-news decision desks. Absentee and provisional ballots can take days to count in some jurisdictions. The previous day’s Supreme Court order concerned a specific procedural question in California and does not itself decide any race.

Readers comparing early vote totals to 2022 or 2024 should note differences in state laws on when and how early ballots are reported. A higher early total in one state does not automatically translate to a higher final turnout percentage nationwide.

What this story is not

It is not a declaration of which party will control either chamber. It is not evidence that any particular ballot was invalid. The Capitol photograph is an exterior of the building where the new Congress will convene in January 2027; it is not a picture of voters or of any campaign event on 3 November.
