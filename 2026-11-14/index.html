FAA lifts nationwide ground stop after air-traffic communications overload

The Federal Aviation Administration ordered a nationwide ground stop of departing flights on Friday afternoon after legacy telecommunications equipment at air route traffic control centers became overloaded with data, affecting communications and radar. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy and Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin stated there was no evidence of a cyberattack or intentional act. The stop was lifted at approximately 8:15 p.m. Eastern Time after roughly four to five hours. Thousands of flights were delayed or cancelled; recovery is expected to extend into the weekend. The official statements confirm the overload of old routers and switches; independent verification of the exact failure sequence remains with the FAA investigation. Eileen Laubacher defeats Lauren Boebert in Colorado’s 4th District

The Associated Press and other outlets projected on Friday that Democrat Eileen Laubacher, a retired Navy rear admiral, defeated Republican incumbent Lauren Boebert in Colorado’s 4th Congressional District. With most ballots counted, Laubacher led approximately 48.8 percent to 47.9 percent. The district voted for Donald Trump by 18 points in 2024. Boebert conceded and called Laubacher to congratulate her. Campaign finance reports show Laubacher raised substantially more than Boebert. The vote totals and projection are from election officials and the AP; characterizations of the broader midterm wave are interpretive. Tanker collides with docked river cruise boat in New Orleans; all accounted for

On Friday around 12:30 p.m. local time the tanker Atlantic Asphalt, flagged to the Marshall Islands, collided with the docked American Cruise Lines paddlewheeler American Heritage on the Mississippi River near the Port of New Orleans. The cruise vessel rolled onto its side and took on water as passengers were disembarking. Mayor Helena Moreno and other officials stated that all approximately 180–190 people aboard were accounted for; 32 were taken to hospitals with non-critical injuries. Coast Guard and local authorities said the tanker appeared to have lost steering control. The cause remains under investigation by the NTSB and Coast Guard. Campaña electoral del 29N: líderes piden movilización y presentan propuestas

La campaña para las elecciones generales del 29 de noviembre continúa en España. Pedro Sánchez ha pedido en actos públicos una participación del 75 % para alcanzar 176 escaños. Alberto Núñez Feijóo y Santiago Abascal realizan mítines centrados en vivienda, economía y cambio político. Las encuestas publicadas sitúan al PP en primer lugar, seguido del PSOE y Vox, con el bloque de derecha cerca de la mayoría absoluta en varias mediciones.
