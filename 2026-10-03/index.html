A judge barred Big Bend barrier work unless environmental laws are met

Friday’s preliminary injunction, reported by Reuters and the Associated Press, tells the government not to build barrier or related infrastructure, and not to disturb ground, in the Big Bend sector unless environmental laws are followed. It arrives about seven weeks after Commissioner Rodney Scott paused work for a visit. CBP has described the park piece as roads, sensors, and vehicle barriers, not the 30-foot walls used elsewhere. The Times says federal lawyers had told the court that work outside the park would resume. Plaintiffs are likely to succeed on at least some claims, Cardone wrote; that is not a final merits ruling. Jordahl’s “total victory” and the department’s confidence in its authorities are quotes. The canyon photograph is from 2006, not Friday’s site.

David Rivera was sentenced to 10 years in the Venezuela lobbying case

Judge Melissa Damian imposed 10 years on Friday after a May conviction for money-laundering conspiracy and failing to register as a foreign agent. Prosecutors described a $50 million contract with a PDVSA affiliate and about $20 million in personal proceeds; forfeiture of that second figure is still pending. Esther Nuhfer received five years. Rubio and Sessions testified they were not told of the contract. That testimony is not a charge against them. Rivera’s “death sentence” remark is a plea tied to age and cancer, not a medical finding in the judgment. A pardon application and an appeal are defense plans, not grants. The Capitol frame is the building he served in, not the Miami hearing.

Attorneys said Christa Pike was unconscious after a failed execution

Counsel said Friday that Pike, the only woman on Tennessee’s death row, was critically ill, unconscious, and on a ventilator after Wednesday’s attempt, with injuries to both arms from pentobarbital. They describe two doses, about an hour of attempts, at least seven needles, and one needle bent near 90 degrees. The claim that the drug entered tissue because lines were misplaced is a filing theory, marked by the word “apparently,” not a published medical-examiner finding. The Times reconstruction uses witnesses and the lawyer at Riverbend and calls this the second execution pause in five years. No state vital-signs table appears in the pieces scored here. The Capitol still is a government building, not the prison and not the prisoner.

Hochul assigned Letitia James to the Cornell investigation

The new institutional fact is the transfer of the case. Hochul said Thursday night she had lost faith in the Tompkins County district attorney and named Attorney General James, who may investigate and present to a grand jury. The allegation itself remains the September civil suit: a 19 October 2024 encounter at Chi Phi, reported weeks later to Cornell police, with no charges then. Van Houten had said he would reopen; CNN says he welcomed the superseding appointment. Hochul’s “literally gang raped” sentence is an argument about procedure, not a verdict. The BBC keeps “alleged.” CNN’s “national outrage” is a frame. The tower is a campus landmark, not the house.
