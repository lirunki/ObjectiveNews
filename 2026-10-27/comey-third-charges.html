Washington · courts · 27 October 2026

Justice Department unseals criminal information charging James Comey with 2016 national-defense disclosure.

ObjectiveNews. Target T 100 · N 0 · C 100.

What is agreed across desks

On 26 October 2026 federal prosecutors in the District of Columbia unsealed a criminal information charging former FBI Director James B. Comey with transmitting national defense information and conspiring to transmit such information. The document, filed earlier in the month under seal, alleges that in October 2016 Comey directed staff to speak with a New York Times reporter about U.S. email-surveillance practices in an effort to correct what he viewed as misinformation in a prior Reuters report. The Times article in question appeared on 5 October 2016 under the headline concerning Yahoo’s adaptation of a spam filter.

AP, Reuters, the BBC, the Washington Post and CNN all report that this is the third criminal case brought against Comey by the Trump administration’s Justice Department since 2025. The first, involving alleged false statements to Congress, was dismissed by a judge. A second case concerning an Instagram post remains pending. Comey’s attorney Patrick Fitzgerald stated that the new allegations had been investigated previously by different teams and did not result in charges. Comey posted a video saying “I’m still innocent.”

The Justice Department has described the information as a mechanism to toll the ten-year statute of limitations on the Espionage Act offense, which expired earlier in October. Officials noted that filing an information for this purpose has been upheld by circuit courts. The document is not yet an indictment returned by a grand jury.

What is only a quote

Comey’s assertion of innocence and Fitzgerald’s claim that the case is legally invalid are statements by the defense. They are not findings. Prosecutors’ description of the 2016 events as an illegal transfer of classified information is an allegation contained in the information. No public transcript of the underlying classified material has been released, so the content of what was allegedly disclosed remains unverified outside the charging document.

Mechanism a reader needs

A criminal information is a charging document signed by a prosecutor rather than returned by a grand jury. In federal practice it can be used when the defendant waives indictment or, as claimed here, to stop the running of a statute of limitations. The Espionage Act (18 U.S.C. § 793) criminalizes the unauthorized communication of national-defense information. The ten-year limitations period begins on the date of the alleged act. Tolling means pausing that clock so that a later indictment can still be filed.

Comey served as FBI Director from 2013 until his dismissal by President Trump in May 2017. The 2016 period falls under his tenure. The Times article concerned a program in which Yahoo modified a spam filter to scan incoming email for specific content at the request of U.S. intelligence. Whether any classified details were actually confirmed to the reporter is the factual core of the allegation; the wires report the existence of the allegation but do not independently confirm the content of any conversation.

Previous prosecutions of Comey were brought by different U.S. Attorneys’ offices and involved different statutes. Dismissal of the false-statements case turned on the appointment of the special counsel. Those procedural histories do not automatically control the present information.

What this story is not

It is not a conviction. It is not a finding that classified information was in fact disclosed. It is not evidence that the statute of limitations has been successfully tolled until a court rules.
